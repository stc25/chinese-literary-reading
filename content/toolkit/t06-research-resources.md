## Summary

Full-text databases, digitised sources and Cambridge library resources for late-Qing and Republican texts.

## Full-text databases

| Resource | Contents | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Chinese Text Project (ctext.org) | pre-modern texts, with OCR of many scanned editions and a dictionary | free |
| Wikisource (zh.wikisource.org) | transcriptions of many texts used in this course; quality varies, so check the edition notes | free |
| 漢籍電子文獻資料庫 (Scripta Sinica), Academia Sinica | standard histories, including the 清史稿, and much else | partly free |
| 中國基本古籍庫 (愛如生) | about 10,000 pre-modern titles with images | subscription |
| 中國近代史數據庫 (Academia Sinica, MHDB) | late Qing and Republican documents, including archives and periodicals | partly free |
| HathiTrust, Internet Archive, Google Books | scans of nineteenth- and early twentieth-century books, Chinese and Western | free, some restricted |

Search the full text, then **always check a hit against a scan**: OCR and transcriptions contain errors,
and many were converted from simplified characters (see [Script and dictionaries](t01-script-and-dictionaries.md)).

## Newspapers and periodicals

| Resource | Contents |
|---|---|
| 《申報》 (Shenbao, 1872–1949) | the leading Shanghai daily; several full-text and image databases exist |
| 全國報刊索引 (CNBKSY): 晚清期刊全文數據庫 and 民國時期期刊全文數據庫 | thousands of periodicals, 1833–1949, including 新民叢報, 東方雜誌, 新青年 |
| 大公報, 北華捷報 (*North-China Herald*) | other major papers, in database editions |
| 政府公報 and 臨時政府公報 | government gazettes; the source of the documents in [Republican documents](../units/u14-republican-documents/unit.md) |
| MHDB 近代婦女期刊資料庫 | women's periodicals |

## Gazetteers

Local gazetteers (方志) survive for almost every county, prefecture and province, often in several editions.

- 中國方志庫 (愛如生) and the 中國數字方志庫: full text and images (subscription).
- 《中國地方志集成》: the main reprint series, in many libraries.
- Harvard-Yenching Library and the Library of Congress have digitised many gazetteers (free).
- For how to read an entry, see [Fishing Mountain](../units/u18-scholarship-and-history/u18-p03-fishing-mountain.md).

## Biographical databases

- **China Biographical Database (CBDB)**: names, offices, kinship and social ties, mostly before 1911.
- 中央研究院〈人名權威資料查詢〉: names, courtesy names and styles.
- 清代檔案人名權威資料 (National Palace Museum, Taipei): Qing officials in the archives.
- Reference works in English: Hummel, *Eminent Chinese of the Ch'ing Period*; Boorman, *Biographical
  Dictionary of Republican China*.

See also [Names, titles and offices](t03-names-titles-offices.md).

## Cambridge resources

- **Cambridge University Library**: the Chinese collections (Aoi Pavilion) hold the main reprint series,
  gazetteers, standard histories and many late-Qing and Republican books and periodicals. Search
  **iDiscover** in Chinese characters (traditional or simplified) or pinyin; older records may use Wade–Giles.
- **Faculty of Asian and Middle Eastern Studies Library**: teaching collections and reference works.
- **Chinese Studies LibGuide**: the current list of subscription databases available to Cambridge readers,
  with access instructions.
- **Needham Research Institute**: history of science and technology in China.
- For material not held in Cambridge, use inter-library loan, or the collections of SOAS, the Bodleian and
  the British Library.

## Practice

### 1. short-answer

You find a sentence of Liang Qichao on Wikisource and want to quote it in an essay. What should you do before
citing it?

**Answer:** Check it against a scan of the original printing or a reliable edition, and cite that edition, not the
transcription, since transcriptions can contain errors and conversion mistakes.
