## Summary

How characters are built, the variant forms found in period printing, and how to look up an unknown character.

## How characters are built

Most characters combine a **radical** (部首), which suggests the meaning, with a **phonetic**,
which suggests the sound: 河 hé "river" = 氵 "water" + 可 kě. The phonetic is often only a rough
guide, since the sounds have changed over three thousand years. The radical is the key to
traditional dictionaries: the 214 radicals of the 康熙字典 (1716) are still the standard order.

Learn to see the parts of a character. If you can name its radical and count its remaining
strokes, you can find it in any traditional dictionary.

## Variant forms

Period printing uses many forms that differ from the standard traditional forms in modern
dictionaries and fonts. The course keeps the forms of its sources and glosses them as "= standard
form".

| In period texts | Standard traditional | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 爲 | 為 | to be, for |
| 眞 | 真 | true |
| 卽 | 即 | then, is |
| 旣 | 既 | since, already |
| 敎 | 教 | to teach |
| 衞 | 衛 | to guard |
| 靑 | 青 | blue-green |
| 說 / 説 | 說 | to speak |
| 决, 减 | 決, 減 | to decide, to reduce |
| 箇 | 個 | measure word |
| 坿 | 附 | to attach |
| 礮 | 砲 / 炮 | cannon |
| 閒 | 間 | between (also read xián "leisure") |
| 景 | 影 | shadow (an older usage) |

Some writers go further and prefer old or rare forms on principle: Zhang Binglin writes 逹 for 達,
㑹 for 會, 冣 for 最 (see [Good and evil both evolve](../units/u17-thought-and-translation/u17-p03-good-and-evil-both-evolve.md)).

**Taboo forms (避諱).** Characters in an emperor's personal name were avoided in the Qing, usually by
writing a near form or leaving out a stroke: 玄 (Kangxi, 玄燁) → 元; 弘 (Qianlong, 弘曆) → 宏; 寧
(Daoguang, 旻寧) → 甯. A substitution of this kind helps to date a printing.

**Errors in digital texts.** Online transcriptions are often converted from simplified-character
editions, and the conversion goes wrong where one simplified character stands for several
traditional ones. Typical errors:

| Simplified | Possible traditional forms | Typical error |
|---|---|---|
| 后 | 後 "after", 后 "empress" | 皇後 for 皇后 |
| 余 | 餘 "remainder", 余 "I" | 其余 for 其餘 |
| 系 | 係, 繫, 系 | 維系 for 維繫 |
| 云 | 雲 "cloud", 云 "to say" | 詩雲 for 詩云 |
| 干 | 幹, 乾, 干 | 乾涉 for 干涉 |
| 发 | 發, 髮 | 頭發 for 頭髮 |
| 几 | 幾, 几 "small table" | 機下 or 幾下 for 几下 |

When a character looks wrong in a digital text, consider whether it is a conversion error and check
a scan. Several passages in this course record such corrections in their review notes.

## Dictionaries

| Dictionary | Use |
|---|---|
| 《康熙字典》 (1716) | the standard character dictionary of the Qing; arranged by radical; gives classical readings and uses |
| 《辭源》 (1915) and 《辭海》 (1936) | the first modern Chinese dictionaries of words and phrases; good for period usage, allusions and new terms |
| 《漢語大詞典》 (1986–94) | the largest dictionary of Chinese words, with dated quotations; the first place to look for the history of a word |
| 《漢語大字典》 (1986–90) | the largest dictionary of characters, including variants |
| Mathews, *Chinese-English Dictionary* (1931) | compiled from period texts; Wade–Giles; good for words of the 1900s–1920s |
| Giles, *A Chinese-English Dictionary* (2nd ed., 1912) | large, with many official and classical terms |
| Kroll, *A Student's Dictionary of Classical and Medieval Chinese* | concise, for classical senses |
| 教育部《異體字字典》 (online) | identifies variant forms and their standard equivalents |
| Pleco (app) | quick look-up, with add-on classical dictionaries |

Remember that modern dictionaries give modern senses first. For a word in a text of 1900, check the
period sense (see [New terms](../grammar/neologisms.md)).

## Looking up a character

1. **By pronunciation**, if you can guess it from the phonetic: type pinyin into Pleco or an online
   dictionary and choose from the list.
2. **By radical and strokes**: identify the radical, count the remaining strokes, and use the
   radical index of a printed dictionary or the radical search of an app.
3. **By handwriting**: draw the character in Pleco or a handwriting input. Stroke order matters less
   than you might fear.
4. **By four-corner code (四角號碼)**: many indexes of the 1920s–80s (including those to names and
   place names) are ordered by this numeric system. The code is given in most large dictionaries.

## Typing Chinese

Use a traditional-character input method (pinyin with traditional output, or 注音 Zhuyin) so that your
notes match your sources. Do not convert simplified text to traditional automatically: it produces
the errors listed above. For scanned sources, OCR tools now handle traditional print well, but check
every result against the scan.

## Practice

### 1. choice

A digital text reads 皇後下詔. What has probably happened?

- [ ] 後 is a period variant of 后.
- [x] The text was converted from simplified characters, and 后 "empress" became 後 "after".
- [ ] It is a taboo substitution.

### 2. short-answer

Give the standard traditional forms of 卽, 旣 and 爲.

**Answer:** 即, 既, 為.
