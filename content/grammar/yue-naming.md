## Summary

Besides "to say", 曰 introduces a name or an item in a list: "is called", "namely".

## Explanation

曰 has three common uses:

1. **"to say"**, introducing speech or a quotation: 任公曰 "Rengong says".
2. **"is called"**, after a description and before a name:
   - 至高無上曰天 "what is highest, with nothing above it, is called 天";
   - 夜見西方曰長庚 "(when) it appears in the west at night it is called Changgeng".
3. **"namely"**, introducing numbered items in a list:
   - 一曰保守，二曰進取 "the first is conservation, the second is advance".

**Word order.** In use 2 the description comes first and the name **last**, the reverse of
an English definition. Find the name after 曰, then read the description: "天 is what is
highest…".

**To call something by a name.** 曰 can also mean "to call (it)": 一則曰老大帝國 "first
they call it 'the old empire'".
