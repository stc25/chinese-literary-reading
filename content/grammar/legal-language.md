## Summary

The vocabulary and sentence patterns of laws and regulations.

## Explanation

**A small set of words does most of the work.** Laws, regulations and treaties of this
period, Qing and Republican, share a drafting vocabulary. Learn these and you can read the
structure of almost any article.

| Word | Pinyin | Function | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 凡 | fán | "all, every": sets the scope of a rule | 凡陸軍軍人及軍屬人員，一律按章發給俸餉 |
| 均, 一律 | jūn, yīlǜ | "all alike, without exception" | 中華民國人民一律平等 |
| 得 | dé | "may": grants a power or permission | 臨時大總統得宣告戒嚴 |
| 不得 | bùdé | "may not": prohibits | 非依法律不得逮捕 |
| 應, 須 | yīng, xū | "shall, must": imposes a duty | 但須提交參議院議决 |
| 但 | dàn | "provided that, except that": a proviso (但書) | 但大赦須經參議院之同意 |
| 其…者 | qí … zhě | "those (of them) who …": picks out a case | 其願入學堂者 |
| …者 | zhě | "whoever …": the subject of a penal rule | 但限滿之後尚未遷徙者 |
| 依, 按, 照 | yī, àn, zhào | "according to" | 依法律, 按章 |
| 有…時 | yǒu … shí | "when there is …" | 有認爲…必要時 |
| 經…之同意 | jīng … zhī tóngyì | "with the consent of" | 經參議院之同意 |
| 左列 | zuǒliè | "the following" (listed to the left, in vertical text) | 左列各項之自由權 |
| 本章, 本約, 本法 | běn … | "this chapter, treaty, law" | 本章所載人民之權利 |
| 前條, 前項 | qiántiáo, qiánxiàng | "the preceding article, paragraph" | |

**The shape of an article.**

1. **Scope**: who or what (凡…, 人民…, 臨時大總統…).
2. **Rule**: a power (得), a duty (應, 須), a prohibition (不得), or a statement (有…之權).
3. **Condition or method**: 依法律, 經…同意, 有…時.
4. **Proviso**: 但… introduces the exception.

**Numbering.** 第…條 "article", 第…章 "chapter", 第…款 "clause" (in treaties, "article"),
第…項 "paragraph", and items numbered 一、二、三.

**Precision and vagueness.** Legal Chinese often leaves out the subject (who deems it
necessary? who decides?). When you translate a law, notice what the text does **not** say.

| 臨時大總統經參議院之同意，得宣戰、媾和及締結條約 | línshí dàzǒngtǒng jīng Cānyìyuàn zhī tóngyì, dé xuānzhàn, gòuhé jí dìjié tiáoyuē | the Provisional President may, with the consent of the Senate, declare war, make peace and conclude treaties | u15-p01 |
