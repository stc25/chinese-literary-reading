## Summary

以 introduces the means, instrument or reason of an action ("with, by means of, because of"), and in fixed patterns a purpose ("so that") or a role ("as").

## Explanation

以 + noun stands **before** the verb and says *how* or *with what* the action is done:

- 赫以死罪 "threatened (them) with death";
- 准以洋銀六百萬圓償補原價 "agrees to compensate the original value with six million silver dollars".

**Common patterns.**

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 以 A V | do V with / by means of A | 以洋銀…償補 |
| 以 A 為 B | take A as B, regard A as B | 西人以此為聯句屬文之要規 "Westerners regard this as the essential rule…" |
| V A 以為 B | V A as B | 索出鴉片以為贖命 "demanded opium as ransom" |
| …，以便 V | so that, in order to V | 給予沿海一處，以便修船 "give a place on the coast so that (they) can repair ships" |
| 以 + clause | because | (later units) |

**以 after a verb phrase.** 以 can also link two verb phrases, "in order to": V₁ 以 V₂ "do V₁ so as to
V₂". The verb before 以 is the means, the one after it the aim.

**以 in compounds.** You will often meet 以 in words for time and place: 以後 "after", 以前
"before", 以上 "above", 以下 "below". Here 以 has lost its separate meaning.
