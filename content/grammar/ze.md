## Summary

則 introduces the consequence of a condition ("then"), what follows at a given stage, or a contrast.

## Explanation

則 stands at the start of the second part of a sentence, **after** the subject if there is one,
and links it to what comes before:

**1. Condition and result: "(if) A, then B".** Often there is no word for "if"; 則 alone shows
that the first part is a condition:

- 夜不炳燭則昧 "(if) at night you do not light a candle, (then) it is dark";
- 委心任運，聽其流變，則日趨於敝 "if one gives up and lets things drift, then day by day they decay".

With a marked condition: 藉曰不變，則…息矣 "supposing they did not change, then … would cease".

**2. Time or stage: "when A, then B".**

- 及其成群成國，則群與群爭 "when they come to form groups and states, then group struggles with group".

**3. Discovery: "then (I found that)".**

- 福成於二十八日回錫，則伯兄病已革矣 "when I returned to Wuxi on the twenty-eighth, (I found
  that) my brother's illness had become critical".

**4. Contrast, in parallel clauses: "as for A…, as for B…".** 勤則壽，逸則夭 "the diligent
live long, the idle die young". Each 則 marks what holds for its topic.

**Translating.** "Then" often sounds heavy in English. Use "if…", "when…", or simply a comma.

## Examples

| Chinese | Pinyin | Translation | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 夜不炳燭則昧，冬不御裘則寒 | yè bù bǐngzhú zé mèi, dōng bù yùqiú zé hán | at night, if you do not light a candle, it is dark; in winter, if you do not wear furs, it is cold | u09-p02 |
| 藉曰不變，則天地人類并時而息矣 | jièyuē bù biàn, zé tiāndì rénlèi bìngshí ér xī yǐ | supposing they did not change, heaven, earth and humankind would all cease at once | u09-p01 |
| 及其成群成國，則群與群爭，國與國爭 | jí qí chéng qún chéng guó, zé qún yǔ qún zhēng, guó yǔ guó zhēng | when they come to form groups and states, then group struggles with group and state with state | u09-p03 |
| 福成於二十八日回錫，則伯兄病已革矣 | Fúchéng yú èrshíbā rì huí Xī, zé bóxiōng bìng yǐ jí yǐ | when I returned to Wuxi on the twenty-eighth, my brother's illness had become critical | u07-p01 |
| 故勤則壽，逸則夭 | gù qín zé shòu, yì zé yāo | therefore the diligent live long and the idle die young | u08-p03 |
