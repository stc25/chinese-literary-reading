## Summary

而 joins two verbs or clauses: "and", "and so", "and then", "and yet".

## Explanation

而 links **verb phrases and clauses**, not nouns. (Nouns are joined by 與, 及 or 暨.) The
relationship between the two parts is not stated by 而 itself; you work it out from the meaning:

| Relationship | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| sequence, result | 晝夜變而成日 | day and night change and so make a day |
| addition | 自審其敝而自變之 | examine the decay and change it themselves |
| manner | 并時而息 | cease all at once |
| contrast | 渡河而乘陸車者 | one who crosses a river and yet rides a land carriage |
| new turn at the start of a sentence | 而弱者當為強肉 | and so the weak are bound to become meat for the strong |

**A common pattern.** A 而 B often means "do A, and thereby B" or "A, and as a result B":
不再傳而其種盡矣 "before two generations have passed, their kind dies out".

**而 at the start of a clause.** 而 can open a clause that adds a new point or a contrast:
方擬十月初旬出洋，而瘧疾遽發 "just as I was planning to go abroad, **but** malaria broke out".

**Fixed expressions.** 而已 "and that is all", "only" (如是而已 "just this"); 而後 "only then";
然而 "however"; 既而 "soon after".
