## Summary

Ways of saying that something is done to the subject: 爲…所, 見, 被, and 於 marking the agent.

## Explanation

Verbs have no passive form, so the same verb can be active or passive. Often the context
alone tells you: 人民之身體，非依法律不得逮捕 "the person of the people may not be
arrested except according to law". But there are also several markers.

| Pattern | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| verb + 於 + agent | 唾棄於時 | be spurned by one's contemporaries |
| 見 + verb | 見棄 | be cast aside |
| 見 + verb + 於 + agent | 見用於人 | be employed by others |
| 為 + agent + 所 + verb | 為人神所憑依 | be relied on by people and spirits |
| 為 + agent + 所 + verb | 為薄霧所迷 | be led astray by mist (Xue Fucheng's diary) |
| 被 + verb | 被撞 | be struck |
| 被 + verb (in nouns) | 被選舉 | to be elected; 被害人 "the injured party" |

**The agent.** In the 為…所 pattern the agent comes **between** 為 and 所: 為 A 所 V "be V-ed
by A". With 於, it **follows** the verb: V 於 A. With 見 and 被 (in this period), the agent
is usually not named.

**見 is not always passive.** 見 is also the verb "to see" (每見人… "every time I see
people…") and is read xiàn "to appear" (夜見西方). It marks a passive only when it stands
directly before another verb.

**為 has many jobs.** In 為 A 所 V, look for the 所: without it, 為 is probably "to be"
(wéi) or "for" (wèi).

## Examples

| Chinese | Pinyin | Translation | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 無不見用於人，見稱於時 | wúbù jiàn yòng yú rén, jiàn chēng yú shí | without exception they have been employed by others and praised by their contemporaries | u08-p03 |
| 勤則有材而見用，逸則無能而見棄 | qín zé yǒu cái ér jiàn yòng, yì zé wú néng ér jiàn qì | the diligent have talent and are employed, the idle have no ability and are cast aside | u08-p03 |
| 是以君子欲為人神所憑依 | shìyǐ jūnzǐ yù wéi rén shén suǒ píngyī | so, if the noble person wishes to be relied on by people and spirits | u08-p03 |
