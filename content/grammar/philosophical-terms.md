## Summary

Key terms of Chinese philosophy and how reformers reused them for Western ideas.

## Explanation

**Why these words matter.** Writers of the 1890s–1910s learnt their philosophy from the
Confucian classics and from Neo-Confucianism (理學, the philosophy of Zhu Xi 朱熹 and his
school). When they met Western ideas, they reached first for these words. A term like 理 or 性
in a text of 1900 may carry its old sense, a new Western sense, or both. Read it in context, and
do not assume the modern Chinese meaning.

**The core terms.**

| Term | Pinyin | Traditional sense | Uses in the new learning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 道 | dào | the Way: the right order of the world and of conduct | a general principle; 人道 "the human way, society"; 群道 "social order" |
| 理 | lǐ | principle, the pattern in things (Neo-Confucian *li*) | a law or principle of science (公理 "universal law", 物理 "physics"); 理性 "reason" |
| 氣 | qì | vital energy, the stuff of things | matter, energy, gas (氣體); 形氣 "the physical world" |
| 性 | xìng | human nature, the nature of a thing | nature in general (天地之性); the suffix "-ity" (理性, 個性) |
| 心 | xīn | the heart-mind, seat of thought and feeling | mind; 心理 "psychology" |
| 天 | tiān | heaven, the order above humans | nature (天演 "evolution", 天擇 "natural selection") |
| 群 | qún | the flock; humans' ability to form groups (Xunzi) | society (群學 "sociology", 保群 "preserving the group") |
| 公 / 私 | gōng / sī | public and impartial / private and selfish | the public good (公益, 公德) / the individual, self-interest (自營) |
| 仁 | rén | benevolence, humaneness | sympathy, altruism |
| 德 | dé | virtue, moral power | virtue, a quality; 道德 "morality" |

**Pairs.** Many terms come in pairs, and the pair is the argument:

| Pair | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 體 / 用 | substance / function | 以天演為體，而其用有二 "evolution is the substance, and it has two functions" |
| 本 / 末 | root / branch | 執其末以齊其本 "takes the branch and equates it with the root" |
| 實 / 用 | reality / use | 進化之實不可非，而進化之用無所取 |
| 因 / 果 | cause / effect | 倒果為因 "takes the effect for the cause" |
| 公 / 私 | public / private | 由仁勝私 "by benevolence the self is overcome" |
| 形 / 景 | form / shadow | 如景之隨形 "like a shadow following its form" |

The reform slogan 中學為體，西學為用 "Chinese learning as substance, Western learning as function"
uses the first pair. When you meet one member of a pair, look for the other.

**Old words as translations.** Yan Fu chose old terms for Western concepts deliberately: he
wanted Western ideas to read as continuous with Chinese thought.

| Western term | Yan Fu | Later standard term |
|---|---|---|
| evolution | 天演 | 進化 |
| struggle for existence | 物競 | 生存競爭 |
| natural selection | 天擇 | 自然選擇, 自然淘汰 |
| society | 群 | 社會 |
| sociology | 群學 | 社會學 |
| economics | 計學 | 經濟學 |
| self-assertion, self-interest | 自營 | 利己 |
| conscience | 天良 | 良心 |

Most of Yan's words lost out to Japanese coinages (see [New terms](neologisms.md)), but they
show how a Chinese reader of 1900 first understood the ideas.

**Reading tips.**

- Check whether 天 means "heaven" (with moral overtones) or simply "nature".
- 理 can be "principle" in the Neo-Confucian sense or "reason, argument" (其理至為要妙).
- 群 used as a verb means "to form groups" (能群者存，不群者滅).
- 性 at the end of a compound is often the new suffix "-ity, -ness": 理性 "reason", 個性
  "individuality".
