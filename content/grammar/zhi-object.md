## Summary

After a verb or preposition, 之 means "him, her, it, them".

## Explanation

When 之 follows a verb (or a preposition such as 與 "with") and **nothing** follows it in
the phrase, it is an object pronoun:

- 爾可從之切磋 "you may go to him (之) to polish your skills" (之 = Chen Jimu);
- 爾須與之互相勸勉 "you must encourage each other with them" (之 = the two cousins);
- 人固有之 "people indeed have it (this)".

之 can refer to a person, a thing, or a whole situation just described, as in 謂之
"call it…" (see [謂之: "is called"](weizhi.md)).

**Three uses of 之.** This is the third main use of 之 in the course. The test is what
comes right after it:

| What follows 之 | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a noun, ending the phrase | joins modifier and noun | 寫字之法 "the method of writing" |
| a verb, often with an object | between subject and verb | 日本人之稱我中國也 |
| nothing (or the next verb of a series) | object pronoun | 從之 "follow him" |

See [之 joining modifier and noun](zhi-attributive.md) and [之 between subject and verb](zhi-nominaliser.md).

**Singular or plural.** 之 can be "him", "her", "it" or "them"; only the context tells you.
