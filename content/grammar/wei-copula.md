## Summary

As a verb, 爲 means "to be", "to act as" or "to become", and in the pattern 以…爲… "to take … as …".

## Explanation

**Two forms.** 爲 and 為 are the same character. Period texts use both, and this course
keeps the form of the source edition.

**Read wéi** (second tone) in these uses. As a verb between two nouns, 爲 has several
related meanings:

| Meaning | Example |
|---|---|
| to be | 動者為行星 "those that move are planets" |
| to become | 死海之瀦爲澤 "the Dead Sea pooling into a marsh" |
| to take as, to serve as | 月借日光為光 "the moon borrows the sun's light as (its) light" |

**爲 versus 也.** Both can make "A is B". With 爲, the sentence has a verb, like English "is";
with 也, it is a nominal sentence (see [也 ending a nominal sentence](ye-final.md)).
爲 tends to be used when B is a category A belongs to, or something A turns into.

**以 A 爲 B "take A as B".** 西人以此為聯句屬文之要規 "Westerners take this as the essential
rule for joining phrases and composing writing". See [以](yi-preposition.md).

**Do not confuse** with 爲 read **wèi** "for, on behalf of", taught in
[爲: "for, on behalf of"](wei-preposition.md).

## Examples

| Chinese | Pinyin | Translation | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 動者為行星，不動者為恆星 | dòng zhě wéi xíngxīng, bù dòng zhě wéi héngxīng | those that move are planets; those that do not move are fixed stars | u02-p02 |
| 月借日光為光 | yuè jiè rìguāng wéi guāng | the moon borrows the sun's light as its own light | u01-p02 |
| 西人以此為聯句屬文之要規 | xīrén yǐ cǐ wéi liánjù zhǔwén zhī yàoguī | Westerners regard this as the essential rule for joining phrases and composing writing | u01-p03 |
| 老年人如死海之瀦爲澤 | lǎoniánrén rú Sǐhǎi zhī zhū wéi zé | the old are like the Dead Sea that has pooled into a marsh | u05-p02 |
