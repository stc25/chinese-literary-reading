## Summary

New words of the period, many borrowed from Japanese, for new concepts and institutions.

## Explanation

**A flood of new words.** Between about 1895 and 1915 Chinese took in thousands of new terms
for Western ideas, institutions and things. Contemporaries called them 新名詞 "new terms" or
新學語 "new learned terms". Some welcomed them; others mocked them. By the 1920s most had become
ordinary modern Chinese.

**Where they came from.**

| Source | How | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Missionary and Qing translations (from the 1840s) | coined in China, often for science and technology | 電報 telegraph, 火車 train, 輪船 steamship, 化學 chemistry |
| Old words, new senses (often via Japanese) | a classical word reused for a Western concept | 文明 civilisation, 革命 revolution, 經濟 economy, 國民 citizen, 自由 freedom |
| Japanese coinages from characters (*wasei kango*) | compounds made in Japan in the 1860s–1900s | 哲學 philosophy, 社會 society, 主義 -ism, 學科 discipline, 方面 aspect |
| Chinese coinages that lost out | invented by Yan Fu and others, later replaced | 天演 (→ 進化) evolution, 群學 (→ 社會學) sociology, 計學 (→ 經濟學) economics |
| Transliterations | sound-based | 德律風 telephone, 德謨克拉西 democracy, 烏托邦 utopia |

**Reading old words with new meanings.** The hardest cases are words that look classical. In a
text of 1900, check whether a word is used in its old or its new sense:

| Word | Old sense | New sense |
|---|---|---|
| 國家 | the state and the ruling house | the nation-state |
| 革命 | change of dynastic mandate | revolution |
| 經濟 | ordering the world and helping the people (經世濟民) | economy |
| 文明 | brilliance, cultured | civilisation |
| 社會 | a village festival association | society |
| 自由 | doing as one likes (often negative) | freedom, liberty |

**Tell-tale features of the new vocabulary.**

- **Two-character compounds**, where classical Chinese would use one character (Wang Guowei:
  日本人多用雙字).
- **Suffixes** that make abstract nouns: 主義 "-ism", 性 "-ity", 的 "-ic" (as in 實際的, 科學的),
  化 "-isation", 界 "world, circles" (文學界), 家 "-ist" (史家, 政治家).
- **Words for new things**: 火車, 輪船, 電報, 分鐘.

**Finding the meaning.** Dictionaries of modern Chinese give the modern sense. For the period
sense, use the 漢語大詞典 (which dates first uses), Lydia Liu's lists of loanwords in
*Translingual Practice* (1995), and contemporary glossaries such as 《新爾雅》 (1903).
