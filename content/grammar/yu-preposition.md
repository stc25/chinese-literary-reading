## Summary

於 introduces place, time, recipient or the standard of comparison: "at, in, to, from, than".

## Explanation

於 (also written 于) is the most general preposition. Its meaning depends on the verb:

| Use | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| time | 於道光十九年二月間 | in the second month of the 19th year of Daoguang |
| place | 立於世界 | stand in the world |
| direction, recipient | 請願於議會 | petition (to) the assembly |
| source | 受於… | receive from… |
| comparison | 大於地球 | bigger than the earth |
| agent of a passive | 見笑於人 | be laughed at by people (see [Passive constructions](passives.md)) |

**Position.** Unlike most prepositional phrases, a 於-phrase of place, recipient or
comparison usually **follows** the verb: 請願於議會 "petition to the assembly", 大於地球
"bigger than the earth". A 於-phrase of time may come **before** the verb, as in the treaty:
大憲等於道光十九年二月間…強留 "the officials … in the 19th year of Daoguang … detained".

**Comparison.** Adjective + 於 + noun means "more … than": 大於地球百四十倍 "a hundred and forty times
bigger than the earth" (from the 1901 primer).

**Watch the boundaries.** 於 often follows another word with which it does not form a
compound: in 大憲等於道光十九年, 等 "and others" belongs to the preceding list, and 於 starts the time phrase.
