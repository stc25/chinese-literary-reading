## Summary

者 after a verb phrase means "the one who …" or "that which …"; it turns the phrase into a noun.

## Explanation

者 follows a verb or a whole verb phrase and turns it into a noun phrase for the **doer**
(a person or a thing):

- 殺人者 "one who kills a person, whoever kills a person";
- 動者 "those that move"; 不動者 "those that do not move";
- 能耐艱苦者 "those who can endure hardship".

The phrase before 者 can be long: 有一材一技、能耐艱苦者 "those who have some talent or
skill and can endure hardship". In unpunctuated text, look for where the description
begins (often after 凡 "all", 有 "there are", or a comma) and end it at 者.

**者 in laws.** Penal codes and regulations are built on it: "X 者，處 Y" "whoever does X
shall be sentenced to Y". 但…者，不在此限 "but … (cases) where …, this does not apply".

**Other uses of 者.**

| Use | Example | See |
|---|---|---|
| marks the term in a definition: A 者，B 也 | 衝突者，調和之先驅也 | [A 者 B 也](zhe-ye-definition.md) |
| closes 所以…者 "the reason why…" | 人之所以貴於他物者 | [所以](suoyi.md) |
| 有…者 "there are some who…" | 徃徃有損壞須修補者 | — |

**者 and 所.** 者 points to the doer; 所 + verb points to the thing done. See
[所 + verb: "that which"](suo.md).
