## Summary

The fixed wording of imperial edicts and rescripts.

## Explanation

**Kinds of imperial document.**

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| 上諭 shàngyù, 諭旨 | an edict, the emperor's own instruction |
| 旨 zhǐ | a decision, often in reply to a memorial |
| 詔 zhào | a solemn proclamation to the whole empire (明定國是詔, the abdication edict of 1912) |
| 懿旨 yìzhǐ | an edict of the Empress Dowager |
| 硃批 zhūpī | a "vermilion rescript" written by the emperor on a memorial |

**The frame of an edict.**

| Part | Formulas | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| date and channel | 某年月日內閣奉上諭 | "the Grand Secretariat received the following edict" |
| occasion | 某某奏…一摺 | "X has memorialised on …": the memorial being answered |
| the emperor's voice | 朕, 朕惟 | "We", "We consider" |
| orders | 著…, 著即…, 著照所請 | "let …", "let … at once", "let it be as requested" |
| prohibitions | 不得…, 毋… | "may not …" |
| close | 將此通諭知之, 欽此 | "let this be proclaimed to all"; "respect this" |

**著 zhuó.** The key verb of edicts: "let (someone) do", "it is ordered that". 著軍機大臣議奏
"let the Grand Councillors deliberate and report"; 著改為民政部 "let it be renamed the Ministry
of Civil Affairs". Do not confuse it with 著 zhù "to write" or the aspect marker 著.

**欽此 qīncǐ.** "Respect this": the last words of an imperial document. When an official
quotes an edict, 欽此 marks where the quotation ends, and he often adds 欽遵 "respectfully
complying". 欽 also marks things that belong to the emperor: 欽差 "imperial commissioner",
欽定 "imperially authorised".

**Rescripts.**

| Rescript | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 知道了 | "noted" |
| 依議 | "as proposed" |
| 該部議奏 | "let the Board concerned deliberate and report" |
| 著照所請 | "let it be as requested" |
| 另有旨 | "a separate decision will follow" |

**The emperor's tone.** Edicts praise (用意至為深遠), warn (不得敷衍因循) and judge, often
cautiously (所陳不為無見 "not without insight"). They are written by Grand Councillors in the
emperor's name; the first person 朕 does not mean he wrote it himself.
