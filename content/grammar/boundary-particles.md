## Summary

Particles that begin or end sentences, such as 夫 and 蓋 at the start and 也, 矣, 焉, 哉 at the end, and help you divide a text.

## Explanation

**Particles at the start.** These open a sentence or a new point. In unpunctuated text, put a break **before** them:

| Particle | Job | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 夫 fú | opens a general statement or a new point: "now…" | 夫楚當春秋魯文、宣之際 "now, Chu at the time of…" |
| 蓋 (盖) gài | introduces an explanation or a supposition: "for…", "it seems…" | 是語也，盖襲譯歐西人之言也 |
| 且 qiě | adds a further point: "moreover" | |
| 故, 是以 | draws a conclusion: "therefore" | 故夫變者，古今之公理也 |
| 然, 然則 | turns: "but", "in that case" | 然則自由之義… |

**Particles at the end.** These close a sentence or a clause. Put a break **after** them:

| Particle | Job | See |
|---|---|---|
| 也 | statement, definition, judgement; also after a topic | [也 ending a nominal sentence](ye-final.md), [也 after a topic](ye-topic.md) |
| 矣 | change, new situation, result | [矣: a new situation](yi-final.md) |
| 焉 yān | statement with a sense of "there, in it"; sometimes emphasis | 而弱者當為強肉，愚者當為智役焉 |
| 哉 zāi | exclamation, rhetorical question | [Question particles](question-particles.md) |
| 乎, 耶, 歟 | questions | [Question particles](question-particles.md) |
| 耳, 而已 | "only, that is all" | 以其能群耳 |

**Caution.** 也 can close a topic **inside** a sentence (昔楚莊王之霸也，…), so a break after 也
is not always a full stop. 焉 is also a question word ("how?") at the start of a clause. 夫
also means "man, husband" (read fū) when it is a noun.

## Examples

| Chinese | Pinyin | Translation | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 夫楚當春秋魯文、宣之際 | fú Chǔ dāng Chūnqiū Lǔ Wén, Xuān zhī jì | now Chu, at the time of Dukes Wen and Xuan of Lu in the Spring and Autumn period | u11-p01 |
| 語其禍，則共工之狂、辛有之痛，不足喻也 | yù qí huò, zé Gònggōng zhī kuáng, Xīn Yǒu zhī tòng, bùzú yù yě | to speak of the danger: even the madness of Gonggong or the grief of Xin You is not enough to illustrate it | u11-p01 |
| 而弱者當為強肉，愚者當為智役焉 | ér ruò zhě dāng wéi qiángròu, yú zhě dāng wéi zhìyì yān | and so the weak are bound to become meat for the strong, and the foolish servants of the clever | u09-p03 |
| 是語也，盖襲譯歐西人之言也 | shì yǔ yě, gài xíyì Ōuxīrén zhī yán yě | this expression, it seems, is a borrowed translation of what Europeans say | u05-p01 |
