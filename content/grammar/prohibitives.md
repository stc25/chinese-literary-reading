## Summary

勿 and 毋 express prohibitions: "do not", "must not".

## Explanation

勿 and 毋 stand before a verb and forbid the action:

- 舅勿來 "uncle, do not come".

The person told not to act is often named first, as here (舅 "uncle"), or left unstated.

**After verbs of hoping and urging.** 毋 (and 勿) also follow verbs such as 冀 "hope",
囑 "urge", 戒 "warn", giving "hope that … will not", "urge … not to":

- 冀弟毋動肝氣 "I hope you will not lose your temper".

**In rules and laws.** In regulations you will meet 毋得 "must not" alongside 不得 "may not";
in letters and instructions, 切勿 "on no account".

**Compare.** 不 states that something does not happen; 勿 and 毋 tell someone not to do it:

| Sentence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 舅不來 | uncle does not / will not come |
| 舅未來 | uncle has not come yet |
| 舅勿來 | uncle, do not come |

**弗** is a third word you will sometimes meet in the same sense, and also as a strong "not".
