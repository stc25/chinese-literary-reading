## Summary

不 is the ordinary negative placed before verbs and adjectives.

## Explanation

不 "not" stands directly before the verb, adjective or verb phrase it negates:

- 不受上級官廳之干涉 "are not subject to interference from higher offices";
- 余不能與弟相見 "I cannot see you" (不 + 能 "can");
- 不動者為恆星 "those that do not move are fixed stars".

**Common combinations.** 不 often joins a following word to make a fixed expression:

| Expression | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 不能 | cannot |
| 不得 | may not, must not (in laws and rules) |
| 不同 | different (lit. "not the same") |
| 不外 | to be nothing other than |

**Which negative?** Literary Chinese has several words for "not", each with its own job:

| Word | Job | See |
|---|---|---|
| 不 | plain "not" with verbs and adjectives | this page |
| 未 | "not yet", "has not" | [未: "not yet"](wei-not-yet.md) |
| 無 | "there is no", "to have no" | [有 and 無: existence](you-wu.md) |
| 非 | "is not" (identity) | [非: "is not"](fei-negation.md) |
| 勿, 毋 | "do not" (forbidding) | [勿 and 毋: "do not"](prohibitives.md) |
