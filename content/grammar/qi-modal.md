## Summary

Before a verb, 其 can express doubt ("can it really be that…?") or urging ("let…"), without referring to anything.

## Explanation

Besides the pronoun "his, its, their" (see [其](qi-pronoun.md)), 其 has a second use:
placed **directly before the verb phrase**, it adds a tone to the whole sentence.

**Doubt, in questions: "can it really be that…?"**

- 我中國其果老大矣乎 "has our China *really* grown old?" (Liang doubts it).

Often with 果 "really" or 豈 "how could". The question is usually rhetorical.

**Urging, in appeals and commands: "let…, may…, do…"**

- 其慎勿毒自由以毒天下也 "*let them* take care not to poison freedom, and thereby poison the world".

**How to tell it from the pronoun.**

| 其 is… | When | Example |
|---|---|---|
| the pronoun "his, its, their" | a noun or the subject of a clause follows, and you can ask "whose?" | 輕棄其鄉 "abandon their home" |
| the pronoun "it, he" inside a clause | after 如, 及, 知 etc., as subject | 如其老大也 "if it is old" |
| a modal particle | it stands between the subject and the verb of a question or an appeal, and refers to nothing | 我中國其果老大矣乎 |

If replacing 其 with "his/its/their" makes no sense, read it as doubt or urging.
