## Summary

How foreign names and places were written in characters, and how to identify them.

## Explanation

**Characters for sound.** Foreign names were written with characters chosen for their sound,
regardless of their meaning: 阿明 Āmíng for Amiens, 福克司登 Fúkèsīdēng for Folkestone. Before
the 1920s there was no standard system. The same name could be written many ways (采令克路司 and
車林克洛司 for Charing Cross), and many transliterations reflect Cantonese, Fujianese or Shanghai
pronunciations rather than Mandarin.

**Recognising a transliteration.**

- The characters make no sense as words: 采令克路司 "pick-order-overcome-road-manage".
- Common sound-characters recur: 阿, 巴, 布, 德, 爾, 克, 拉, 蘭, 里, 利, 羅, 馬, 納, 尼, 士, 斯, 司, 亞, 伊.
- A classifier word follows: …國, …洲, …江, …城, …島, …海口 (亞細亞洲, 尼羅江, 錫蘭島, 布倫海口).
- The context calls for a name: 抵… "arrived at …", 曰… "called …", …公使 "minister …".

**Kinds of name.**

| Kind | Examples |
|---|---|
| countries and continents | 英吉利, 法蘭西, 意大里, 亞細亞洲, 歐羅巴洲, 阿非利加洲, 亞美理駕洲 |
| short forms | 英, 法, 德, 美, 俄, 意: the first character of the full name, used alone |
| cities and places | 倫敦 London, 巴黎 Paris, 柏林 Berlin, 布倫 Boulogne |
| people (full transliteration) | 華盛頓 Washington, 林肯 Lincoln, 卜魯士 Bruce, 斯土活 Stowe |
| people (Chinese-style names) | 利瑪竇 Matteo Ricci, 威妥瑪 Thomas Wade, 巴夏禮 Harry Parkes |
| words | 德律風 telephone, 加非 coffee, 伯理璽天德 president |

**Chinese-style names.** Westerners who lived in China took names that looked Chinese: a
one-character surname (often the first syllable of the real surname) and a one- or
two-character given name. 威妥瑪: 威 for Wade, 妥瑪 for Thomas. These are fixed names, used in
Chinese sources for that person only, so look them up rather than reconstructing them.

**Identifying a name.**

1. Read it aloud in Mandarin, then think of Cantonese or southern pronunciations (e.g. 士 for *-s*).
2. Use context: nationality, office, date, place.
3. Check a reference: 《近代來華外國人名辭典》 for people in China; atlases of the period; and
   other translations of the same text.
4. Do not trust the author too far: [Xue Fucheng got Ricci's name the wrong way round](../units/u16-the-press-and-new-vocabulary/u16-p03-paris-to-london.md).
