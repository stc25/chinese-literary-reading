## Summary

Words before the verb that show whether an action is complete, past experience, about to happen or in progress.

## Explanation

Verbs have no tenses. Time is shown by dates and time words (昨日, 今, 明年…), by the order
of clauses, and by a small set of adverbs placed **before the verb**:

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 已 yǐ | already (done) | 已購船票矣 "(I) had already bought the tickets" |
| 既 jì | once, since (something is done) | 朝與國既異物 "since a dynasty and a state are different things" |
| 嘗 cháng | once, at some time in the past; "have ever" | 未嘗 "have never" |
| 將 jiāng | about to, will | 考試將近 "the examinations are approaching" |
| 方 fāng | just then, just as; in the course of | 方擬十月初旬出洋 "just as I was planning to go abroad in the tenth month" |
| 正 zhèng | right now, at its height | 時疫正盛 "the epidemic is at its height" |

**Sequences.** Narratives link events with words such as 遂 "thereupon", 乃 "then, only then",
始 "only then, for the first time", 忽 "suddenly": 方擬…，忽接…來電 "just as I was planning…, I
suddenly received a telegram". 未 "not yet" is the negative counterpart of 已 (see [未](wei-not-yet.md)).

**Translating.** Choose the English tense from the context of the whole passage. 已 in a
narrative about the past is "had already"; in a present situation, "has already".

**將 has other jobs.** 將 also means "to take" and can bring the object before the verb:
將香港一島給予大英君主 "give the island of Hong Kong to the British sovereign". If a noun follows
將 and a second verb comes after it, read it this way.

## Examples

| Chinese | Pinyin | Translation | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 則伯兄病已革矣 | zé bóxiōng bìng yǐ jí yǐ | (I found that) my brother's illness had become critical | u07-p01 |
| 方擬十月初旬出洋，而瘧疾遽發 | fāng nǐ shíyuè chūxún chūyáng, ér nüèjí jù fā | just as I was planning to go abroad in the first ten days of the tenth month, I suddenly came down with malaria | u07-p02 |
| 已購船票矣 | yǐ gòu chuánpiào yǐ | I had already bought the tickets | u07-p02 |
| 德法時疫正盛 | Dé Fǎ shíyì zhèngshèng | the epidemic is at its height in Germany and France | u07-p02 |
| 又弗以考試將近而間斷看書之課 | yòu fú yǐ kǎoshì jiāngjìn ér jiànduàn kànshū zhī kè | nor interrupt your daily reading because the examinations are approaching | u07-p03 |
| 朝與國既異物 | cháo yǔ guó jì yìwù | since a dynasty and a state are different things | u05-p03 |
