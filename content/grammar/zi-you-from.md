## Summary

自 and 由 mark the starting point in place or time ("from"); 由 also introduces who does something ("by").

## Explanation

**自 "from".** 自 + time or place, before the verb:

- 自今以後 "from now on";
- 自壬寅年起至乙巳年止 "from the renyin year to the yisi year".

The pattern **自 A 起 至 B 止** "starting from A, ending at B" is common in documents for
periods of time. Shorter forms are 自 A 至 B and 由 A 至 B.

**由 "from" and "by".** 由 can mean "from" like 自, but in documents it often introduces
**the person or body that does something**, "by":

- 中華民國之憲法，由國會制定 "the constitution of the Republic of China shall be drawn up
  by the parliament";
- 由中國官為償還 "to be repaid by the Chinese officials".

**自 is not always "from".** Directly before a verb or modal, 自 means "oneself, by oneself" or
"naturally": 自應給予 "should naturally be given", 自定 "decide for oneself". Ask whether
a time or place follows 自: if not, it is probably not "from".
