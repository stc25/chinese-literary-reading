## Summary

其 refers back to a person or thing and stands before a noun or a verb: "his", "its", "their".

## Explanation

其 is the pronoun for "his, her, its, their". It always stands **before** what it goes with:

- before a noun: 輕棄其鄉 "lightly abandon their home districts";
- before a verb phrase, as its subject: 吾見其寄岱雲信 "I saw his-sending-to-Daiyun
  letter", i.e. "the letter he sent to Daiyun".

In the second use 其 works like "he" or "it", but only *inside* a larger phrase; it does not
begin an independent sentence the way English "he" does.

**Finding the referent.** 其 usually points back to the topic or subject of the sentence,
or to the person being discussed: in 仕宦之家…輕棄其鄉, "their" is the officials' families.

**其 and 之.** 其 comes before; 之 "him, it, them" comes after a verb or preposition.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| "his / him" | 其寄岱雲信 "the letter he sent" | 從之 "follow him" |

**Other uses of 其.** Before a verb in a question or a wish, 其 can express doubt or urging
without referring to anyone: 我中國其果老大矣乎 "has our China *really* grown old?". See
[其 expressing doubt or urging](qi-modal.md).
