## Summary

Before a noun and a verb, 爲 (read wèi) means "for" or "on behalf of"; it can also mean "because of".

## Explanation

爲 (also written 為) has two readings and two main jobs:

| Reading | Job | Example |
|---|---|---|
| wéi | verb "to be, to act as" | 動者為行星 "those that move are planets" (see [爲: "to be, to act as"](wei-copula.md)) |
| wèi | preposition "for, on behalf of" | 由中國官為償還 "the Chinese officials will repay (it) on their behalf" |

As a preposition, 爲 stands **before** the verb, with or without its object:

- with an object: 爲 A V "do V for A";
- without one, as in 為償還, when it is clear from context for whom the action is done.

**"Because of".** 爲 wèi can also give a reason: 為英國事被拿監禁 "imprisoned because of
British affairs". The reading and meaning are the same family: "for the sake of".

**How to choose the reading.** If 爲 links two nouns ("A 爲 B") or ends a phrase with a
noun after it as complement, it is usually wéi. If 爲 + (noun) is followed by **another
verb**, it is usually wèi: 為 + 償還.

## Examples

| Chinese | Pinyin | Translation | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
