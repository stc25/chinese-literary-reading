## Summary

Final particles that make a question or an exclamation: 乎 (yes or no), 耶/邪 and 歟/與 (often either–or or doubtful), 哉 (exclamation, rhetorical question).

## Explanation

A statement becomes a question by adding a particle at the end. Word order does not change.

| Particle | Typical use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 乎 hū | yes-or-no question | 世有愛自由者乎？ "are there people who love freedom?" |
| 耶 yē (also 邪) | questions, often with doubt; each half of an either–or question | 為老大耶？為少年耶？ "is it old? is it young?" |
| 歟 yú (also 與) | questions inviting agreement: "is it not…?" | — |
| 哉 zāi | exclamation; rhetorical question | 何一非…之陳跡哉！ "which of it is not a trace of…!" |

**Combinations.** 矣乎 asks about a change: 我中國其果老大矣乎 "has our China really grown
old?" 也哉 and 也耶 add emphasis to a rhetorical question.

**V 乎不 V？** "V or not?": 能乎不能？ "can he or can he not?". In this period the form is
usually rhetorical, expecting "no".

**With a question word, no particle is needed.** 夫國也者，何物也？ "now, what is a state?"
The particle may still be added for emphasis.

**乎 is also a preposition.** Inside a sentence 乎 can mean "at, in, than", like 於: 不外乎是 "nothing other than this". At the end of a sentence it is a question particle.

## Examples

| Chinese | Pinyin | Translation | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 我中國其果老大矣乎 | wǒ Zhōngguó qí guǒ lǎodà yǐ hū | has our China really grown old? | u10-p01 |
| 欲斷今日之中國為老大耶？為少年耶？ | yù duàn jīnrì zhī Zhōngguó wéi lǎodà yē? wéi shàonián yē? | if we want to decide whether the China of today is old or young | u10-p01 |
| 然則自由之義，竟不可行以個人乎 | ránzé zìyóu zhī yì, jìng bùkě xíng yǐ gèrén hū | in that case, can freedom after all not be applied to the individual? | u10-p02 |
| 能乎不能 | néng hū bù néng | can he or can he not? | u10-p03 |
| 何一非我國民少年時代良辰美景、賞心樂事之陳跡哉 | hé yī fēi wǒ guómín shàonián shídài liángchén měijǐng, shǎngxīn lèshì zhī chénjì zāi | which of it is not a trace of the fine days and delights of our nation's youth! | u10-p03 |
