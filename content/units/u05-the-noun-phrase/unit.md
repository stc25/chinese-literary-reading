## Introduction

之 is one of the most frequent characters in literary prose. This unit sorts out its main jobs inside the noun phrase.

## Objectives

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- distinguish 之 joining a modifier to a noun from 之 between subject and verb
- read clauses used as topics
- read 其 as the subject of a clause
