## Introduction

The opening of the Provisional Constitution of 1912, which you read in
[Verbs, objects and negation](../u03-verbs-and-negation/unit.md), as it appeared in the
government gazette: the president's order promulgating it, his name and the date, and the
first four articles. This passage opens in the **vertical, unpunctuated** view.

The text is easy; the point is the layout. In vertical text, read each column from **top to
bottom**, and the columns from **right to left**. The first line of the page is the one on the far right.

## Text

玆准參議院咨送議决臨時約法前來，合行公布。

孫文

中華民國元年三月十一日

第一條：中華民國由中華人民組織之。

第二條：中華民國之主權屬於國民全體。

第三條：中華民國領土，爲二十二行省、內外蒙古、西藏、靑海。

第四條：中華民國以參議院、臨時大總統、國務院、法院行使其統治權。

## Translation

Having received the Provisional Constitution passed by the Senate and sent to me, I hereby promulgate it.

Sun Wen

11 March, first year of the Republic of China

Article 1: The Republic of China is constituted by the Chinese people.

Article 2: The sovereignty of the Republic of China belongs to the whole body of citizens.

Article 3: The territory of the Republic of China consists of the twenty-two provinces, Inner and Outer Mongolia, Tibet and Qinghai.

Article 4: The Republic of China exercises its governing power through the Senate, the Provisional President, the cabinet and the courts.

## Notes

**Reading vertical text.** Start at the top right. Read down the first column, then move
**left** to the next. A page of the 1912 gazette has the order (玆准…公布) in the first columns,
the president's name and the date in their own columns, and then the articles, each
starting a new column.

**Layout replaces punctuation.** With no punctuation, the layout does part of the job:

- each article begins a new column, after its number (第一條);
- the signature and the date stand apart;
- inside an article, you divide the text by meaning, as in [Finding the breaks](u11-p01-finding-the-breaks.md).

**Lists.** 參議院臨時大總統國務院法院 is a list of four institutions, which the edition
separates with 、. Knowing the names (參議院, 大總統, 國務院, 法院) is what lets you divide it: see
[Names, titles and offices](../../toolkit/t03-names-titles-offices.md).

**Dates.** 中華民國元年 "the first year of the Republic" is 1912. From 1912 the Republic used
the Western calendar, so 三月十一日 here is 11 March, not a lunar date. See
[Dates and calendars](../../toolkit/t02-dates-and-calendars.md).

**The promulgation order.** 玆准…咨送…前來，合行公布 "having received … sent by …, I hereby
promulgate it" is a formula of official correspondence: you will meet it again in
[The Republican state](../u14-republican-documents/unit.md).

## Exercises

### 1. choice

In vertical text, in which order are the columns read?

- [ ] left to right
- [x] right to left, each from top to bottom
- [ ] bottom to top

### 2. punctuate

Punctuate article 4.

**Text:** 中華民國以參議院臨時大總統國務院法院行使其統治權

**Answer:** 中華民國以參議院、臨時大總統、國務院、法院行使其統治權。

**Explanation:** The four institutions form a list, separated by 、.

### 3. punctuate

Punctuate article 3.

**Text:** 中華民國領土爲二十二行省內外蒙古西藏靑海

**Answer:** 中華民國領土，爲二十二行省、內外蒙古、西藏、靑海。

### 4. short-answer

What tells you, in the original, where each article begins?

**Answer:** Each article starts a new column with its number (第一條, 第二條…).
