## Introduction

In January 1912 the revolutionaries set up a Provisional Government in Nanjing, with Sun
Yat-sen as Provisional President. Its gazette, the 臨時政府公報, printed the president's
orders (令), the ministries' petitions (呈) and their rulings (批).

During the fighting, troops had occupied the city's schools and confiscated buildings, and
books and instruments had been burnt or carried off. The Ministry of Education petitioned the
president; the president then ordered the Ministries of War and of the Interior to help.
This order contains the petition inside it. The first task is to find **where the
petition begins and ends**, and where the president's own words begin.

## Text

據敎育部呈：「竊南京自光復以後，凡學堂局所及充公房屋等處，恆為兵隊駐紮，所有房屋、器物、書籍、儀器等多遭焚燬、搬取，損失甚鉅，公家財產措辦匪易，亟應加以保護。敝部前經派員分路調查各學堂所存書籍、儀器，隨加封條，以免再遭損失。惟到處仍有軍隊駐紮，恆與調查員齟齬。至充公之房屋內中所有精本書籍亦復不少，調查員前往，亦恆為看守人所拒，敝部權力實有不及，為此呈請大總統令下陸軍部、內務部各派人員，會同敝部調查員前往辦理，庶幾公家保存一分財產，卽社會多培一分元氣。為此呈請大總統即日令行，毌任盻切。」等情前來。

合就令行該部，速派妥員，會同敎育部調查員及內務、陸軍部所派人員，前往各處學堂及前查封充公之家屋內妥愼照料保護，毌任毀壞散失，以重文敎而保公產。此令。

## Translation

I have received a petition from the Ministry of Education: "We humbly report that since the restoration of Nanjing, the schools, offices and confiscated buildings have constantly been occupied by troops, and the buildings, equipment, books and instruments have mostly been burnt or carried off. The losses are very great; public property is not easily provided, and it urgently needs protection. Our ministry previously sent officials by different routes to investigate the books and instruments kept in the schools, and sealed them so as to avoid further losses. But troops are still stationed everywhere and constantly clash with the investigators. As for the confiscated buildings, there are also not a few fine editions in them, but when the investigators go there they are constantly refused by the guards. Our ministry's authority is truly insufficient. For this reason we request the President to order the Ministry of War and the Ministry of the Interior each to send officials to deal with the matter jointly with our investigators, so that for every portion of public property preserved, society may gain a portion of vitality. For this reason we request the President to issue the order this very day; we cannot express how eagerly we await it." So the petition reads.

It is proper that I hereby order the said ministries to send reliable officials quickly to go, jointly with the investigators of the Ministry of Education and the officials sent by the Ministries of the Interior and of War, to the schools and the confiscated houses, and to look after and protect them carefully, not allowing anything to be damaged or lost, so as to respect learning and protect public property. This is the order.

## Notes

**The shape of the order.**

| Part | Words | Whose words |
|---|---|---|
| receipt | 據敎育部呈： | the president |
| petition quoted | 「竊南京自光復以後 … 毌任盻切。」 | the Ministry of Education |
| close of quotation | 等情前來 | the president |
| decision | 合就令行該部 … 以重文敎而保公產 | the president |
| close | 此令 | the president |

The original had no quotation marks. You find the petition's end by its last formula
(毌任盻切 "we await eagerly", the words of a petitioner) and the closing 等情前來. See
[Citing earlier documents](../../grammar/embedded-citation.md).

**Who is speaking?** Inside the quotation, the ministry calls itself 敝部 "our humble
ministry" and addresses 大總統 "the President". Outside it, the president calls the
ministries 該部 "the said ministries". Pronouns are rare in official writing: these terms tell
you who is speaking to whom.

**Qing forms in a Republican document.** 竊, 為此呈請, 等情前來 and the elevation of 大總統
all come from Qing official practice. The new government kept the old forms of
correspondence while it replaced the old institutions. What has changed is the vocabulary of
rank: 呈 (to a superior), 令 (to a subordinate), and 大總統 in place of 皇上. See
[Republican document types](../../grammar/document-types.md).

**Closing formulas.** Each document type has its own last words: a 令 ends 此令; a 批 ends
此批; a 呈 ends 須至呈者 or 謹呈. You will meet the last two in [A petition and a ruling](u14-p02-a-petition-and-a-ruling.md).

**卽 and 即.** The transcription uses both forms. 卽 is a variant of 即.

### 3. short-answer

What does the ministry call itself inside the petition, and what does the president call the ministries outside it?

**Answer:** The ministry calls itself 敝部 "our (humble) ministry"; the president calls the ministries 該部 "the said ministries".
