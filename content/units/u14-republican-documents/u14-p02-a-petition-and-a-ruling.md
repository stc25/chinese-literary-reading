## Introduction

Two documents from the same issue of the gazette, one going **up** and one going **down**.

The first is a 呈 "petition, report", the form used by anyone writing to a superior: here the
Minister of the Interior reports to the president that he has opened a police school.

The second is a 批 "ruling", the short reply a superior writes on a petition. Local
representatives near Yangzhou had petitioned the Ministry of Industry for permission to
reclaim land on the west shore of Lake Shaobo. The ministry replies: good idea, but first
send us the figures.

## Text

內務總長程德全為呈報事。竊維保民衛國，警務實為行政之樞機；選賢任能，學校尤為儲才之淵藪。查南京現為首善之區，一應警察事宜必先切實改良，方足為各省所表率。惟本部成立伊始，經費困難，凡屬布施，似宜力求撙節，爰將原有之江南高等巡警學堂改為內務部警務學校，先招敎練所學警二百四十人，分科敎授，為急則治標之計，至正班學生，一俟籌有的欵，再行招考敎養，以副大總統謀進人民幸福之本意。並卽委任本部警務局長孫潤宇兼任警務學校校長，以期迅速舉辦，庶於公私兩有裨益。所有開辦警務學校緣由，理應具文，呈請鑒核施行。須至呈者。

據稟邵伯湖西岸地土久荒，該代表等不忍棄利於地，籌辦墾殖，誠不可緩。惟該岸共有地積若干，應計畆、繪圖，先行呈部，以便飭交揚州民政長確查。仰該代表等迅卽遵照，並妥立招股章程辦法，一同呈部。此批。

## Translation

Cheng Dequan, Minister of the Interior, reports as follows. I humbly consider that for protecting the people and defending the state, police affairs are the key to administration; and for choosing the worthy and employing the able, schools are the place where talent is gathered. I note that Nanjing is now the capital, and all police matters must first be thoroughly reformed before it can serve as a model for the provinces. But this ministry has only just been founded and funds are difficult, so all measures should, it seems, be as economical as possible. I have therefore converted the existing Jiangnan Higher Police School into the Ministry of the Interior Police School. It will first recruit 240 police cadets for the training institute and teach them by subject, as an emergency measure. As for students of the regular course, as soon as definite funds have been raised, they will be admitted by examination and educated, so as to fulfil the President's intention of working to advance the people's happiness. I have also at once appointed Sun Runyu, head of this ministry's police bureau, as principal of the police school concurrently, in the hope of setting it up quickly, so that both public and private interests may benefit. As to the opening of the police school, it is proper to prepare this document and request you to examine it and put it into effect. This petition is necessary.

According to your petition, the land on the west shore of Lake Shaobo has long lain waste, and you representatives cannot bear to leave its profit in the ground and plan to reclaim and cultivate it. This truly cannot be delayed. But you should measure in mu how much land there is on that shore, draw a map and first submit them to the ministry, so that we can pass them on with an order to the civil governor of Yangzhou to verify. You representatives are directed to comply at once, and also to draw up proper regulations and procedures for raising capital, and submit them to the ministry together. This is the ruling.

## Notes

**Up and down.** The direction of a document decides its form and its vocabulary:

| | 呈 (upwards) | 批 (downwards) |
|---|---|---|
| opening | 為呈報事。竊維… | 據稟… "according to your petition" |
| the writer | 本部 "this ministry", or 敝部 (humble) | 本部 "this ministry" |
| the reader | 大總統, never "you" | 該代表等 "the said representatives" (= you) |
| request or order | 呈請鑒核施行 "request you to examine and put into effect" | 仰…遵照 "you are directed to comply" |
| close | 須至呈者 | 此批 |

See [Republican document types](../../grammar/document-types.md).

**仰.** In a document to a subordinate, 仰 yǎng (literally "look up") introduces an order:
the inferior is to "look up" to it. 仰該代表等迅卽遵照 "you representatives are directed to
comply at once". See [Directive language](../../grammar/directive-language.md).

**稟 and 呈.** The heading calls the representatives' document a 呈, but the ruling says 據稟.
A 稟 was the Qing word for a petition from a commoner or a junior official; in 1912 both words were still in use.

**The petition's rhetoric.** The opening pairs 保民衛國 with 選賢任能, and 警務 with 學校: four
characters, then a judgement, twice over. Even a routine report begins with a parallel
couplet. 急則治標 comes from medicine: "when it is urgent, treat the symptoms (標); when it is not, the root (本)".

**首善之區.** The capital, from the 漢書: 建首善自京師始 "goodness begins in the capital".

**A summary at the end.** 所有開辦警務學校緣由，理應具文，呈請鑒核施行 is the Qing memorial
formula (所有…緣由，理合…) in Republican form. Compare [A seal and a rescript](../u13-qing-memorials-and-edicts/u13-p03-a-seal-and-a-rescript.md).

### 3. short-answer

What does the Ministry of Industry ask the representatives to send before it can decide?

**Answer:** The area of the land (measured in mu), a map, and regulations and procedures for raising capital.

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the opening of the petition.

**Text:** 竊維保民衛國警務實為行政之樞機選賢任能學校尤為儲才之淵藪

**Answer:** 竊維保民衛國，警務實為行政之樞機；選賢任能，學校尤為儲才之淵藪。

**Explanation:** Two parallel halves, each a four-character phrase followed by a judgement, separated by a semicolon.

### 5. short-answer

How does the ruling refer to the people it is addressed to?

**Answer:** As 該代表等 "the said representatives", used where English would say "you".
