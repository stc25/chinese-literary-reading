## Introduction

Ornate prose relies on parallel phrasing and on allusions the reader is expected to recognise. The three passages come from writers on translation and literature: Yan Fu on the principles of translation, Lin Shu on why he translated *Uncle Tom's Cabin*, and Wang Guowei on the song lyric. Each builds its argument out of quotations and matched phrases. Learning to trace them, and to use parallelism to divide a sentence, is the skill of this unit.

## Objectives

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- use parallelism to read and divide text
- recognise and trace allusions
- read four- and six-character prose
