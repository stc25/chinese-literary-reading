## Introduction

This unit introduces how literary Chinese works: words are mostly single characters, word class depends on position, and many sentences are built as a topic followed by a comment.

## Objectives

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- recognise a topic and its comment
- read simple nominal sentences ending in 也
- see how one word can work as noun, verb or adjective
