## Introduction

Two more entries from the 1901 primer, for 日 and 月. Paragraphs 1 and 2 are the short
definitions, without the notes on pronunciation that open each entry. Paragraph 3 is the last sentence of the longer
explanation of 月. In the source each entry starts with the character itself as a heading;
here it is followed by a colon.

## Text

日：太陽也。

月：俗名亮月，即太陰也。每一月三十日，月小二十九日。

月借日光為光，故對日一面有光，背日一面無光。

## Translation

日: the sun.

月: popularly called "the bright moon"; that is, the 太陰. Each month has thirty days; a short month has twenty-nine.

The moon borrows the sun's light as its own light; so the side that faces the sun is lit, and the side turned away from the sun is dark.

## Literal translation

Sun: great-yang 也.

Moon: common-name bright-moon, that-is great-yin 也. Every one month thirty day(s); month short, twenty-nine day(s).

Moon borrows sun-light as light; therefore face sun one side has light, back sun one side has-no light.

## Notes

**One character, several meanings.** 日 is both "sun" and "day", 月 both "moon" and
"month". Here all five 月 are on show: the moon (月：…, 亮月, 月借…) and the month
(一月, 月小). The context always decides; numbers such as 三十 and 二十九 are a clear signal for "day" and "month".

**Nouns used as verbs.** 背 is "the back" as a noun. In 背日 "back the sun", it stands
before an object and works as a verb: "to turn one's back on the sun". See
[Word classes and flexibility](../../grammar/word-classes.md).

**Topic and comment.** In 對日一面有光 the topic comes first, 對日一面 "the side facing
the sun", and the comment follows: 有光 "there is light". The topic is not the subject of
有 in the English sense; it is simply what the sentence is about. See
[Topic and comment](../../grammar/topic-comment.md).

**Long and short months.** In the traditional lunar calendar a month has either 30 days
(a "big month", 大月) or 29 (a "small month", 小月). See
[Dates and calendars](../../toolkit/t02-dates-and-calendars.md).

## Exercises

### 1. choice

In 每一月三十**日**, what does 日 mean?

- [ ] the sun
- [x] day
- [ ] daily

**Explanation:** After a number (三十), 日 counts days.

### 2. choice

In **背**日一面無光, what does 背 mean?

- [ ] the back (a noun)
- [x] to face away from (a verb)
- [ ] behind (a direction)

**Explanation:** 背 stands before an object (日), so here it works as a verb: "to turn one's back on the sun". It is parallel to 對日 "to face the sun".

### 3. short-answer

In 對日一面有光, which words are the topic and which are the comment?

**Answer:** The topic is 對日一面 "the side facing the sun"; the comment is 有光 "(there) is light".

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the sentence.

**Text:** 月借日光為光故對日一面有光背日一面無光

**Answer:** 月借日光為光，故對日一面有光，背日一面無光。

**Explanation:** 故 "therefore" begins a new clause. The last two clauses are parallel: 對日一面有光，背日一面無光.

### 5. translate

Translate 月借日光為光。

**Answer:** The moon borrows the sun's light as its own light.

**Explanation:** The first 光 is part of 日光 "sunlight"; the second stands alone: "(as its) light".
