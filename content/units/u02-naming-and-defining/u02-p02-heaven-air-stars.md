## Introduction

Five short definitions from the 1901 primer, taken from the entries for 天 (paragraphs 1–2),
氣 (paragraph 3) and 星 (paragraphs 4–5). Each paragraph is a separate sentence from its entry.

## Text

至高無上曰天。

天，積氣也。

靜則曰氣，動則曰風。

動者為行星，不動者為恆星。

恆星有光，行星無光。

## Translation

What is highest, with nothing above it, is called 天 "heaven".

Heaven is accumulated air.

When it is still it is called 氣 "air"; when it moves it is called 風 "wind".

Those that move are planets; those that do not move are fixed stars.

Fixed stars have light of their own; planets have none.

## Literal translation

Highest, nothing-above, called heaven.

Heaven, accumulated air 也.

Still, then called air; move, then called wind.

Move 者 are planets; not move 者 are fixed-stars.

Fixed-stars have light, planets have-no light.

## Notes

**Three ways to define.** This passage uses three patterns side by side:

| Pattern | Example | Order |
|---|---|---|
| description 曰 name | 至高無上曰天 | name last |
| A，B也 | 天，積氣也 | name first |
| A 為 B | 動者為行星 | name last |

See [曰: "is called", "namely"](../../grammar/yue-naming.md),
[也 ending a nominal sentence](../../grammar/ye-final.md) and [爲: "to be, to act as"](../../grammar/wei-copula.md).

**為 and 爲.** These are two forms of the same character. Texts of the period use both;
this course keeps whichever form the source edition uses.

**者 "those that…".** After a verb, 者 makes a noun phrase: 動者 "those that move", 不動者
"those that do not move". It is taught fully in [者: "the one who, that which"](../../grammar/zhe-nominaliser.md).

**…則… "when…, then…".** 靜則曰氣 "(when it is) still, then (it is) called air". 則 marks what follows from a condition.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

In 至高無上曰天, which word is the name being defined?

- [ ] 至高
- [ ] 無上
- [x] 天

**Explanation:** With 曰, the description comes first and the name last.

### 2. choice

What does **動者** mean in 動者為行星?

- [ ] the movement
- [x] those that move
- [ ] moving stars

**Explanation:** 者 after the verb 動 makes a noun phrase: "those that move".

### 3. short-answer

According to the primer, how do planets differ from fixed stars? Give two differences.

**Answer:** Planets move and have no light of their own; fixed stars do not move and have light.

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the sentence.

**Text:** 動者為行星不動者為恆星

**Answer:** 動者為行星，不動者為恆星。

**Explanation:** The two halves are parallel: 動者為… / 不動者為….

### 5. translate

Translate 靜則曰氣，動則曰風。

**Answer:** When it is still it is called air; when it moves it is called wind.
