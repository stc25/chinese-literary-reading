## Introduction

The entry for 蝕 in the 1901 primer used in [Words and sentences](../u01-words-and-sentences/unit.md).
Paragraph 1 is the short definition; paragraphs 2 and 3 come from the longer explanation.
The sentence on solar eclipses between them is left out.

## Text

蝕：蟲蛀也。

日月隱蔽曰蝕，如蟲蝕草木之葉也。

地球行至日與月相對之間，則地掩蔽日光，不能映射月面，謂之月蝕。

## Translation

蝕: eaten away by insects.

When the sun or the moon is obscured, it is called 蝕 "eclipse", as when insects eat into the leaves of plants.

When the earth moves into the space where the sun and the moon face each other, the earth blocks the sunlight, which cannot shine onto the face of the moon: this is called a lunar eclipse.

## Literal translation

蝕: insect-bore 也.

Sun-moon hidden, called 蝕, like insects eat-into plants 之 leaves 也.

Earth moves-to sun and moon face-each-other 之 space, then earth blocks sunlight, cannot shine-on moon-face; call it moon-eclipse.

## Notes

**Two ways of naming.** Both name a thing *after* describing it:

- **曰**: description + 曰 + name. 日月隱蔽曰蝕 "(when) the sun or moon is obscured (it) is
  called 蝕". See [曰: "is called", "namely"](../../grammar/yue-naming.md).
- **謂之**: description, then 謂之 + name. 謂 is "to call", 之 is "it", referring back to
  everything described: "(one) calls it a lunar eclipse". See [謂之: "is called"](../../grammar/weizhi.md).

In English it is usually more natural to put the name first: "A lunar eclipse is when…".

**蝕 three ways.** The headword 蝕 is defined as 蟲蛀 "eaten by insects". Then 蝕 is
the name of an eclipse (曰蝕), and in 蟲蝕草木之葉 it is a verb, "to eat into". Position
again decides the word class: after a subject (蟲) and before an object (草木之葉), 蝕 is a verb.
See [Word classes and flexibility](../../grammar/word-classes.md).

## Exercises

### 1. choice

In 日月隱蔽**曰**蝕, what does 曰 mean?

- [ ] to say
- [x] to be called
- [ ] and

**Explanation:** 曰 links a description (日月隱蔽) to the name it is given (蝕).

### 2. choice

In ……不能映射月面，**謂之**月蝕, what does 之 in 謂之 refer to?

- [ ] the moon
- [ ] the sun
- [x] the whole situation just described

**Explanation:** 之 is "it", and here "it" is everything the sentence has described: the earth blocking the sunlight from the moon.

### 3. choice

In 如蟲**蝕**草木之葉, what is 蝕 doing?

- [ ] It is a noun: "an eclipse"
- [x] It is a verb: "to eat into"
- [ ] It is the name being defined

**Explanation:** 蝕 stands between a subject (蟲) and an object (草木之葉), so it works as a verb.

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the sentence.

**Text:** 地球行至日與月相對之間則地掩蔽日光不能映射月面謂之月蝕

**Answer:** 地球行至日與月相對之間，則地掩蔽日光，不能映射月面，謂之月蝕。

**Explanation:** 則 "then" starts a new clause; 謂之 introduces the name at the end.

### 5. translate

Translate 日月隱蔽曰蝕，如蟲蝕草木之葉也。

**Answer:** When the sun or the moon is obscured, it is called 蝕 "eclipse", as when insects eat into the leaves of plants.
