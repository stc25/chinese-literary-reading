## Introduction

From 1902 Liang Qichao 梁啟超 published 〈新民說〉 ("On the New Citizen") as a series of essays in his
journal 新民叢報. In the section explaining what he means by 新民 "renewing the people",
he argues that a nation must both keep its own strengths and take in new ones. These
three sentences come from two paragraphs of that section; the text between them is left out.

## Text

今之世非昔之世，今之人非昔之人。

世界上萬事之現象，不外兩大主義：一曰保守，二曰進取。

有衝突則必有調和，衝突者，調和之先驅也。

## Translation

The world of today is not the world of the past; the people of today are not the people of the past.

All the phenomena of the world come down to two great principles: the first is conservation, the second is advance.

Where there is conflict there will necessarily be reconciliation: conflict is the forerunner of reconciliation.

## Literal translation

Now 之 age is-not past 之 age, now 之 people are-not past 之 people.

World-on all-affairs 之 phenomena, not-beyond two great principles: one is conservation, two is advance.

Have conflict, then necessarily have reconciliation; conflict 者, reconciliation 之 forerunner 也.

## Notes

**A 者，B 也.** 衝突者，調和之先驅也 "conflict is the forerunner of reconciliation" is the
classic pattern of definition. 者 marks the term being defined, and 也 closes what it is.
See [A 者 B 也: defining](../../grammar/zhe-ye-definition.md).

**非 "is not".** 今之世非昔之世 "the present age is not the age of the past". 非 negates
an identity, where English uses "is not". Ordinary verbs are negated with 不 instead (不外 "not go
beyond"). See [非: "is not"](../../grammar/fei-negation.md).

**曰 in a list.** 一曰保守，二曰進取 "the first is conservation, the second is advance". 曰
introduces each item of a numbered list. See [曰: "is called", "namely"](../../grammar/yue-naming.md).

**New words.** 現象 "phenomenon" and 主義 "principle, -ism" are among the many new terms that
came into Chinese around 1900, often by way of Japanese. See
[New terms (新名詞)](../../grammar/neologisms.md).

## Exercises

### 1. choice

In **衝突者**，調和之先驅也, what does 者 do?

- [ ] It means "the one who conflicts"
- [x] It marks 衝突 as the term being defined
- [ ] It ends the sentence

**Explanation:** "A 者，B 也" defines A as B: "conflict is the forerunner of reconciliation".

### 2. choice

What does 非 mean in 今之世**非**昔之世?

- [x] is not
- [ ] not yet
- [ ] there is no

**Explanation:** 非 negates a statement of identity: "A is not B".

### 3. short-answer

What are Liang's two great principles?

**Answer:** 保守 "conservation" and 進取 "advance".

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the sentence.

**Text:** 有衝突則必有調和衝突者調和之先驅也

**Answer:** 有衝突則必有調和，衝突者，調和之先驅也。

**Explanation:** 者 closes the term being defined, so a comma follows it; 也 ends the definition.

### 5. translate

Translate 今之世非昔之世，今之人非昔之人。

**Answer:** The world of today is not the world of the past; the people of today are not the people of the past.
