## Introduction

In his essay on freedom in 〈新民說〉 (1902), Liang Qichao asks whether freedom belongs to
the individual or to the group, and answers his own question in favour of the group.
Paragraph 1 is from the middle of the essay; paragraph 2 from its closing lines (part of
the last paragraph is left out). 自由 "freedom", 個人 "individual" and 團體 "group, collective"
were all recent terms.

## Text

然則自由之義，竟不可行以個人乎？曰：惡是何言？團體自由者，個人自由之積也。人不能離團體而自生存，團體不保其自由，則將有他團焉自外而侵之，壓之，奪之，則個人之自由更何有也？

淺學如余，而欲以區區片言單語發明之，烏知其可？……世有愛自由者乎？其慎勿毒自由以毒天下也。

## Translation

In that case, can freedom after all not be applied to the individual? I answer: nonsense, what talk is this? Collective freedom is the sum of individual freedoms. People cannot survive on their own apart from the group. If the group does not protect its freedom, then other groups will come from outside to invade it, oppress it and plunder it, and then what will be left of the individual's freedom?

For one of shallow learning like me to try to explain it in a few paltry words: how can I know that it will do? … Are there people in the world who love freedom? Let them take care not to poison freedom, and thereby poison the world.

## Notes

**Real questions and rhetorical ones.** Liang asks four questions, but only one is a real
request for an answer:

| Question | Kind |
|---|---|
| 竟不可行以個人乎？ "can it not be applied to the individual?" | posed in order to be answered |
| 惡是何言？ "nonsense, what talk is this?" | rhetorical: rejects the idea |
| 則個人之自由更何有也？ "then what will be left of the individual's freedom?" | rhetorical: "nothing" |
| 烏知其可？ "how can I know that it will do?" | rhetorical and modest: "I doubt it" |
| 世有愛自由者乎？ "are there people who love freedom?" | rhetorical: leads into an appeal |

See [Rhetorical questions](../../grammar/rhetorical-questions.md).

**烏 "how".** 烏 is one of several question words for "how, where": 烏知其可 "how could I know it is acceptable?".
Others are 安, 焉 and 何. See [Question words](../../grammar/question-words.md).

**其 urging.** In 其慎勿毒自由以毒天下也, 其 is not a pronoun. Before a verb in an appeal, it
means "let (them)…, may (they)…": "let them take care not to poison freedom". See
[其 expressing doubt or urging](../../grammar/qi-modal.md).

**之 as object, three times.** 侵之，壓之，奪之 "invade it, oppress it, plunder it": each 之 is the
group whose freedom is lost. The repeated verbs build up force.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

What answer does 則個人之自由更**何有**也？ expect?

- [ ] Freedom will be greater.
- [x] Nothing: there will be no individual freedom left.
- [ ] No answer is expected; it is a real question.

### 2. choice

In **其**慎勿毒自由以毒天下也, what does 其 do?

- [ ] It means "its", referring to freedom.
- [x] It urges: "let them take care…".
- [ ] It asks a question.

### 3. choice

What does **烏**知其可 mean?

- [ ] Alas, I know it will do.
- [x] How can I know that it will do?
- [ ] I know where it will do.

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the sentence.

**Text:** 團體自由者個人自由之積也

**Answer:** 團體自由者，個人自由之積也。

**Explanation:** "A 者，B 也" defines the term.

### 5. translate

Translate 世有愛自由者乎？

**Answer:** Are there people in the world who love freedom?
