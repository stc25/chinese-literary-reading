## Introduction

This passage returns to Liang Qichao's 〈少年中國說〉 (1900), which you read in
[The noun phrase](../u05-the-noun-phrase/unit.md). After comparing old people with young,
Liang repeats his opening question, sets out what each answer would mean, and asks a new
question that the rest of the essay will answer: what is a state?

## Text

任公曰：我中國其果老大矣乎？是今日全地球之一大問題也。如其老大也，則是中國為過去之國，即地球上昔本有此國，而今漸澌滅，他日之命運殆將盡也。如其非老大也，則是中國為未來之國，即地球上昔未現此國，而今漸發達，他日之前程且方長也。欲斷今日之中國為老大耶？為少年耶？則不可不先明「國」字之意義。夫國也者，何物也？

## Translation

Rengong says: Has our China really grown old? This is one of the great questions for the whole world today. If it is old, then China is a country of the past: a country that once existed on the earth but is now gradually fading away, whose fate is probably about to run out. If it is not old, then China is a country of the future: a country that has not yet appeared on the earth but is now gradually developing, whose road ahead is still long. If we want to decide whether the China of today is old or young, we must first make clear the meaning of the word 國 "state". Now, what is a state?

## Notes

**Three kinds of question.**

| Kind | Example | How it is marked |
|---|---|---|
| yes or no | 我中國其果老大矣乎？ "has our China really grown old?" | final particle 乎 (with 其 "really") |
| either–or | 為老大耶？為少年耶？ "is it old? is it young?" | 耶 after each alternative |
| question word | 國也者，何物也？ "what is a state?" | 何 "what" |

The question word stands where the answer would go: 國也者，何物也 "a state is *what* thing?".
See [Question particles](../../grammar/question-particles.md) and [Question words](../../grammar/question-words.md).

**其 "can it really be?".** 其 before the verb in 我中國其果老大矣乎 adds doubt; it does not refer to anyone.
Contrast 如其老大也 "if it (China) is old", where 其 is the subject of the clause. See
[其 expressing doubt or urging](../../grammar/qi-modal.md).

**如其…也，則… "if…, then…".** 如 "if" with 也 closing the condition; 則 brings the result.
The two halves (如其老大也… / 如其非老大也…) are exactly parallel.

- [ ] at the start of the question, as in English

- [x] the subject "it" (China): "if it is old"

**Explanation:** Each alternative ends with 耶 and a question mark.
