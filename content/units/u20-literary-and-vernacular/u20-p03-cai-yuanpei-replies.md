## Introduction

Cai Yuanpei had passed the highest Qing examination, become a member of the Hanlin Academy, then
joined the revolutionaries and studied philosophy in Leipzig. As chancellor of Peking University
from 1917 he appointed both radicals and conservatives, on the principle of 兼容並包 "include and
tolerate all". On the day Lin Shu's letter appeared, Cai replied, point by point, in a letter
printed in the university's daily bulletin.

This passage gives his answer to Lin's second charge, that the university was "abolishing the
old books and writing in the common speech". Cai writes in literary Chinese, as Lin does, but
watch for the vernacular and modern words that slip in (是否, 白話的文字, 形式, 內容), and for the
way he turns Lin's own translations into an argument against him.

## Text

琴南先生左右：

……對於第二點，當先為三種考察：（甲）北京大學是否已盡廢古文而專用白話？（乙）白話果是否能達古書之義？（丙）大學少數教員所提倡之白話的文字，是否與引車賣漿者所操之語相等？……

次考察「白話是否能達古書之義？」大學教員所編之講義，固皆文言矣。而上講壇後，決不能以背誦講義塞責，必有賴於白話之講演，豈講演之語，必皆編為文言而後可歟？吾輩少時，讀《四書集註》、《十三經註疏》，使塾師不以白話講演之，而編為類似集註、類似註疏之文言以相授，吾輩豈能解乎？……

又次考察「大學少數教員所提倡之白話的文字，是否與引車賣漿者所操之語相等？」白話與文言，形式不同而已，內容一也。《天演論》、《法意》、《原富》等，原文皆白話也，而嚴幼陵君譯為文言。少仲馬、叠更司、哈德等所著小說，皆白話也，而公譯為文言。公能謂公及嚴君之所譯，高出於原本乎？……

此復，並候著祺。

八年三月十八日，蔡元培敬啟。

## Translation

To Mr Qinnan:

…… Regarding the second point, I should first examine three questions: (a) Has Peking University abolished classical prose entirely and used only the vernacular? (b) Can the vernacular really convey the meaning of the old books? (c) Is the vernacular writing advocated by a minority of the university's teachers the same as the speech used by those who pull carts and sell soup? ……

Next I examine "Can the vernacular convey the meaning of the old books?" The lecture notes compiled by the university's teachers are certainly all in the literary language. But once on the lecture platform, a teacher can never discharge his duty by reciting his notes from memory; he must depend on explanation in the vernacular. Surely the words of a lecture need not all be composed in the literary language before they are acceptable? When we were young and read the *Collected Commentaries on the Four Books* and the *Commentaries and Sub-commentaries on the Thirteen Classics*, if our village teachers had not explained them in the vernacular but had taught us in literary language like the commentaries and sub-commentaries, how could we have understood? ……

Next again I examine "Is the vernacular writing advocated by a minority of the university's teachers the same as the speech used by those who pull carts and sell soup?" The vernacular and the literary language differ only in form; the content is the same. *On Evolution*, *The Spirit of the Laws*, *The Wealth of Nations* and the rest were all written originally in the vernacular, and Mr Yan Youling translated them into the literary language. The novels by Dumas fils, Dickens, Haggard and others are all in the vernacular, and you, sir, translated them into the literary language. Can you say that your translations and Mr Yan's surpass the originals? ……

This is my reply, with best wishes for your writing.

Eighteenth day of the third month of the eighth year. Cai Yuanpei respectfully writes.

## Notes

**Point by point.** Cai sets out Lin's charge as three questions labelled 甲, 乙, 丙, the Heavenly
Stems used as (a), (b), (c), and answers each in turn (次考察……, 又次考察……). Numbered sub-points
with stems were new in Chinese prose, borrowed from legal and academic writing. Compare the
numbered thesis in [Shang and Zhou institutions](../u18-scholarship-and-history/u18-p02-shang-and-zhou-institutions.md).

**Vernacular inside literary prose.** Cai's grammar is literary (固皆文言矣, 豈……歟, 吾輩豈能解乎),
but some words are not:

| Word | Register | Literary equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| 是否 "whether" | vernacular | ……否, ……與否 |
| 白話的文字 | vernacular 的 | 白話之文字 |
| 對於 "regarding" | modern | 於, 至於 |
| 類似 "similar to" | modern | 如, 類 |
| 形式 "form", 內容 "content" | new terms (from Japanese) | 文, 質 |

白話果是否能達古書之義 mixes both registers in one clause: literary 果 "really" and vernacular
是否 "whether". See [Vernacular elements](../../grammar/vernacular-elements.md) and
[Mixed registers](../../grammar/mixed-register.md).

**Turning the argument.** Lin had said the vernacular was the speech of pedlars. Cai answers that
the books Yan Fu and Lin translated were themselves written in their authors' vernaculars (English,
French), and asks whether the classical translations are better than the originals. The question
needs no answer: Lin, who knew no foreign language, could not claim it. It is also a reminder of
how the translations you read in [U12](../u12-parallelism-and-allusion/unit.md) and
[U17](../u17-thought-and-translation/unit.md) were made.

**The frame.** Like Lin, Cai uses the formulas of a letter between equals: 左右 "(to) your
attendants" as the address term, 公 "you, sir", 此復 "this is my reply", 並候著祺 "with good wishes
for your writing" (著 "writing", for a writer), 敬啟 "respectfully writes". The date is by the
Republic's year (八年), which Lin, a loyalist, would not use. See
[Letter formulas](../../grammar/epistolary-formulas.md).

**What happened next.** Lin answered, and published two short stories mocking the reformers. A
few weeks later the May Fourth demonstrations (4 May 1919) made the new culture a national
movement. In 1920 the Ministry of Education ordered primary schools to teach the national language
(國語) in place of literary Chinese.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

What does Cai say is the difference between the vernacular and the literary language?

- [x] They differ only in form; the content is the same.
- [ ] The literary language is richer in content.
- [ ] The vernacular is the speech of pedlars.

### 2. short-answer

How does Cai use Lin Shu's own work against him?

**Answer:** Lin translated novels written in the vernacular of their authors (Dumas fils, Dickens, Haggard) into literary Chinese. Cai asks whether Lin's translations are better than the originals; if not, the vernacular is no worse than the literary language.

### 3. short-answer

Find two vernacular or modern words in Cai's letter, and give a literary equivalent for one of them.

**Answer:** For example 是否 (literary 與否), 白話的文字 with 的 (literary 之), 對於 (literary 於), 類似 (literary 如), 形式 and 內容 (literary 文 and 質).

### 4. translate

Translate 白話與文言，形式不同而已，內容一也。

**Answer:** The vernacular and the literary language differ only in form; the content is the same.

### 5. punctuate

Punctuate this sentence.

**Text:** 公能謂公及嚴君之所譯高出於原本乎

**Answer:** 公能謂公及嚴君之所譯，高出於原本乎？
