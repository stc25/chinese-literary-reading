## Introduction

By the 1910s and 1920s, literary and vernacular Chinese were often mixed, and the choice between them was itself debated.

## Objectives

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- recognise vernacular elements
- read mixed-register prose
- follow the arguments of the language debates
