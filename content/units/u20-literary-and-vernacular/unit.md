## Introduction

By the 1910s and 1920s, literary and vernacular Chinese were often mixed, and the choice between them was itself debated. In this unit you read three texts from that debate: Chen Duxiu's manifesto 〈文學革命論〉 (1917), Lin Shu's open letter to Cai Yuanpei defending the classical language (1919), and Cai's reply. All three are written in literary Chinese, but in different ways: Chen's is full of new terms and the vernacular particle 的; Lin's is purely classical; Cai's lets vernacular words such as 是否 into literary sentences.

## Objectives

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- recognise vernacular elements
- read mixed-register prose
- follow the arguments of the language debates
