## Introduction

A complete memorial, as printed in a provincial government gazette in 1907. In 1906 the
Ministry of Police (巡警部) had been renamed the Ministry of Civil Affairs (民政部), and it
needed a new seal. This memorial reports, step by step, how the seal was made, collected and
brought into use. Nothing could be more routine, and that is the point: thousands of
memorials like this one fill the archives and gazettes, and they all follow the same form.

Look for the frame (奏爲…事, 伏乞…聖鑒, 謹奏), for the earlier documents quoted inside it
(each ending 欽此 or 前來), and at the end for the emperor's reply: 知道了 "noted". The
passage opens in the **unpunctuated** view. Look for the gaps, then divide.

## Text

奏爲啓用臣部印信日期，並查銷木質關防，恭摺仰祈聖鑒事。

光緒三十二年九月二十日，欽奉懿旨：「巡警爲民政之一端，著改為民政部。」等因。欽此。臣等即於是年九月二十九日奏請飭禮部改鑄民政部印信，並暫刋木質關防，以資鈐用，奉旨：「依議。」欽此。當經咨明禮部，並咨送前領光字一百二十一號巡警部銀質印信一顆查銷在案。嗣於光緒三十三年正月十四日准該部咨，以臣部印信業經鑄就，令派員請領前來。當於本月十六日派員前赴禮部，將光字一百二十九號民政部印信一顆領取到部，臣等即於十九日敬謹啓用，並將木質關防查銷。除分咨京外各衙門查照外，所有臣部開用印信日期，理合繕摺具陳，伏乞皇太后、皇上聖鑒。謹奏。

光緒三十三年正月二十二日奉旨：「知道了。」欽此。

## Translation

Memorial concerning the date on which our ministry brought its seal into use, and the checking and cancellation of the wooden seal. In this respectful memorial we look up and pray for Your Majesties' inspection.

On the 20th day of the 9th month of the 32nd year of Guangxu, we respectfully received an edict of the Empress Dowager: "The police are one part of civil administration. Let it be renamed the Ministry of Civil Affairs." Respect this. On the 29th day of the 9th month of that year we requested by memorial that the Board of Rites be ordered to recast a seal for the Ministry of Civil Affairs, and that a wooden seal be carved for the time being for stamping documents, and received the decision: "As proposed." Respect this. We thereupon informed the Board of Rites by letter and sent with it the silver seal of the Ministry of Police previously issued, number 121 in the 光 series, for checking and cancellation, as is on record. Then on the 14th day of the 1st month of the 33rd year of Guangxu we received a letter from the said Board saying that our ministry's seal had been cast, and asking us to send an official to collect it. On the 16th of this month we sent an official to the Board of Rites to collect the seal of the Ministry of Civil Affairs, number 129 in the 光 series, and bring it to the ministry. On the 19th we respectfully brought it into use, and checked and cancelled the wooden seal. Apart from informing all offices in and outside the capital separately by letter so that they may take note, it is proper that we write out a memorial reporting the date on which our ministry began to use its seal. Prostrate, we beg the Empress Dowager's and the Emperor's inspection. Respectfully memorialised.

On the 22nd day of the 1st month of the 33rd year of Guangxu, the decision was received: "Noted." Respect this.

## Notes

**The whole frame.**

| Part | Here |
|---|---|
| summary (奏爲…事) | 奏爲啓用臣部印信日期，並查銷木質關防，恭摺仰祈聖鑒事 |
| narrative, citing earlier documents | 光緒三十二年九月二十日，欽奉懿旨… |
| summary of the matter (所有…) | 所有臣部開用印信日期 |
| formula of submission | 理合繕摺具陳，伏乞皇太后、皇上聖鑒 |
| close | 謹奏 |
| rescript, with its date | 光緒三十三年正月二十二日奉旨：「知道了。」欽此。 |

The 所有… sentence near the end repeats the subject of the summary at the top, closing the
memorial like a frame. See [Memorial formulas](../../grammar/memorial-formulas.md).

**Documents inside the document.** The memorial tells its story through earlier documents,
each with its own closing word:

- the Empress Dowager's edict: 欽奉懿旨 … 等因。欽此。
- the emperor's reply to their first memorial: 奉旨：「依議。」欽此。
- the Board of Rites' letter: 准該部咨 … 前來.

Learn to find where a quoted document starts (欽奉, 奉旨, 准…咨) and ends (欽此, 等因, 前來).
See [Citing earlier documents](../../grammar/embedded-citation.md).

**Rescripts.** The emperor's reply to a memorial was written in vermilion ink (硃批) or
issued as a short decision (旨). The commonest are 知道了 "noted", 依議 "as proposed", 該部議奏
"let the Board concerned deliberate and report". 知道了 is the vernacular 了, one of the few
places where the spoken language appears in Qing official writing.

**Two rulers.** In 1907 the memorial is addressed to 皇太后、皇上: the Empress Dowager Cixi
held the real power. Her edicts are 懿旨, his are 旨 or 上諭.

**Elevation.** The gaps in the original come before 聖鑒, 懿旨, 飭 (an order issued in the
emperor's name), 旨, 皇太后, 皇上 and 奏. See [Elevation (抬頭) and spacing](../../grammar/elevation.md).

**Letters between offices.** 咨 is a letter between bodies of equal rank (the ministry and the
Board of Rites); 飭 an order to a subordinate. Ranks decide the vocabulary. See
[Republican document types](../../grammar/document-types.md).

## Exercises

### 1. choice

What does 知道了 at the end of the memorial mean?

- [ ] The emperor did not know about the matter.
- [x] It is the emperor's rescript: "noted".
- [ ] The ministry has informed other offices.

### 2. short-answer

Find the three earlier documents quoted in the memorial, and the word that closes each.

**Answer:** The Empress Dowager's edict (closed by 等因。欽此。), the emperor's reply 依議 (closed by 欽此), and the Board of Rites' letter (closed by 前來).

### 3. choice

Why is the memorial addressed to both 皇太后 and 皇上?

- [ ] It was sent to two different palaces by mistake.
- [x] The Empress Dowager Cixi shared, and in fact held, imperial power.
- [ ] 皇太后 is another title of the emperor.

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the closing.

**Text:** 所有臣部開用印信日期理合繕摺具陳伏乞皇太后皇上聖鑒謹奏

**Answer:** 所有臣部開用印信日期，理合繕摺具陳，伏乞皇太后、皇上聖鑒。謹奏。

### 5. short-answer

Where in the original are there gaps (elevation), and why?

**Answer:** Before 聖鑒, 懿旨, 飭, 旨, 皇太后, 皇上 and 奏: words for the rulers, their decisions and acts done in their name, and the act of memorialising them.
