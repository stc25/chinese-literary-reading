## Introduction

Periodicals spread a large new vocabulary and a new, more accessible essay style. In this unit you read Liang Qichao's call for a new history in his journal 新民叢報 (1902), Wang Guowei's essay defending the new terms from Japan (1905), and two entries from an envoy's diary full of transliterated names (1890). Together they show the three main sources of new words: old words given new senses, Japanese coinages, and sound-based transliterations.

## Objectives

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- recognise new terms and their sources
- identify transliterated names and places
- read the essay style of reform-era periodicals
