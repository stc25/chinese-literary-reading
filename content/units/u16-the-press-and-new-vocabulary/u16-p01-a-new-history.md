## Introduction

In 1902, in exile in Japan, Liang Qichao founded the fortnightly 新民叢報 "New Citizen", the
most widely read Chinese periodical of its day. In it he serialised 《新史學》 "The New
Historiography", an attack on the dynastic histories and a call for history written for the
nation and its citizens.

Liang's essay style, called 新民體 after the journal, was deliberately accessible. It is
clear, fast and full of feeling, it uses new terms freely, and it repeats and builds up its points.
Look for the new vocabulary: many of these words came into Chinese through Japanese in the
years just before Liang wrote.

## Text

於今日泰西通行諸學科中，為中國所固有者，惟史學。史學者，學問之最博大而最切要者也，國民之明鏡也，愛國心之源泉也。今日歐洲民族主義所以發達，列國所以日進文明，史學之功居其半焉。然則但患其國之無茲學耳，苟其有之，則國民安有不團結，群治安有不進化者？雖然，我國茲學之盛如彼，而其現象如此，則又何也？

一曰知有朝廷而不知有國家。吾黨常言，二十四史非史也，二十四姓之家譜而己。其言似稍過當，然按之作史者之精神，其實際固不誣也。吾國史家，以為天下者君主一人之天下。故其為史也，不過敘某朝以何而得之，以何而治之，以何而失之而已，舍此則非所聞也。

## Translation

Of all the disciplines current in the West today, the only one that China has had of old is history. History is the broadest and most essential of branches of learning, the bright mirror of the nation and the spring of patriotism. Half the credit for the development of nationalism in Europe today, and for the daily progress of the nations in civilisation, goes to history. In that case, the only worry is that a country might lack this discipline; if it has it, how could its citizens fail to unite, or the order of its society fail to progress? Even so, our country's history has flourished like that, and yet the state of things is like this. Why is that?

The first defect: it knows of the dynasty but not of the nation. We have often said that the Twenty-Four Histories are not histories but the genealogies of twenty-four families, nothing more. This may seem somewhat excessive, but judged by the spirit in which the historians wrote, it is certainly not false. Our historians have held that the empire is the empire of one man, the ruler. So when they write history, they do no more than narrate how a certain dynasty won the empire, how it ruled it and how it lost it. Apart from this, nothing concerns them.

## Notes

**New words.** Almost every key term here was new, or newly used, around 1900:

| Term | Meaning | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 學科 | discipline | Japanese *gakka* |
| 國民 | citizen, the nation | old word ("people of a state"), new sense via Japanese *kokumin* |
| 民族主義 | nationalism | Japanese *minzoku shugi*; 主義 "-ism" is a Japanese coinage |
| 文明 | civilisation | old word (易經), new sense via Japanese *bunmei* |
| 進化 | evolution, progress | Japanese *shinka*; compare Yan Fu's 天演 |
| 現象, 精神, 實際 | phenomenon, spirit, reality | Japanese *genshō*, *seishin*, *jissai* |
| 國家 | the state | old word ("state and dynasty"), new sense opposed to 朝廷 |

Many "Japanese" terms are old Chinese words that Japanese translators gave new, Western
meanings (文明, 國民, 革命); others were coined in Japan from Chinese characters (學科, 主義).
In both cases they came back to China through books and journals like this one. See
[New terms (新名詞)](../../grammar/neologisms.md).

**朝廷 and 國家.** Liang's whole argument rests on a new distinction. In older usage 國家 often
meant the dynasty itself; Liang uses it for the nation-state, which belongs to its citizens
(國民), and opposes it to 朝廷, the court of one family. Compare
[The dynasty and the state](../u05-the-noun-phrase/u05-p03-dynasty-and-state.md), where he
makes the same point.

**The 新民 style.** Notice the series of definitions (史學者…者也，…也，…也), the parallel
rhetorical questions (國民安有不團結，群治安有不進化者), the contrast 如彼…如此, and the
numbered points (一曰…). The grammar is literary Chinese; the vocabulary and the energy are
new. See [Mixed registers](../../grammar/mixed-register.md).

**知有…而不知有….** "To know of A but not of B" is the frame of all four of Liang's "defects":
知有朝廷而不知有國家, 知有個人而不知有群體, 知有陳跡而不知有今務, 知有事實而不知有理想. A
repeated frame like this is a mark of the period's essays.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

What distinction does Liang draw in 知有朝廷而不知有國家?

- [ ] between the capital and the provinces
- [x] between the ruling dynasty and the nation
- [ ] between officials and the emperor

### 2. short-answer

Find four new terms (新名詞) in the first paragraph.

**Answer:** Any four of 學科, 史學, 國民, 愛國心, 民族主義, 發達, 文明, 團結, 進化, 現象.

### 3. short-answer

What does Liang mean by calling the dynastic histories 二十四姓之家譜?

**Answer:** That they are the family records of the ruling houses, about how each dynasty won, ruled and lost power, not histories of the nation.

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the sentence.

**Text:** 史學者學問之最博大而最切要者也國民之明鏡也愛國心之源泉也

**Answer:** 史學者，學問之最博大而最切要者也，國民之明鏡也，愛國心之源泉也。

**Explanation:** A topic with 者, followed by three definitions, each ending with 也.

### 5. translate

Translate 吾國史家，以為天下者君主一人之天下。

**Answer:** Our historians have held that the empire is the empire of one man, the ruler.
