## Introduction

The opening of a letter to both of Zeng's sons, Jize 紀澤 and Jihong 紀鴻. Their
mother is travelling west, up the Yangzi, and Zeng wants the family to go back to their
home district in Hunan rather than stay in Hubei (鄂). He worries that official families
lose touch with their roots.

## Text

爾侍母西行，宜作還里之計，不宜留連鄂中。仕宦之家，往往貪戀外省，輕棄其鄉，目前之快意甚少，將來之受累甚大。吾家宜力矯此弊。

## Translation

You are accompanying your mother on the journey west: you should plan to return to our home village and not linger in Hubei. Families of officials often cling to other provinces and lightly abandon their home districts: the pleasure now is very small, the trouble later very great. Our family must do its utmost to correct this fault.

## Literal translation

You attend mother west-travel, should make return-village 之 plan, not should linger Hubei-in. Official 之 families often cling-to other provinces, lightly abandon their home; present 之 pleasure very small, future 之 trouble very great. Our family should strongly correct this fault.

## Notes

**其 "their".** In 輕棄其鄉, 其 means "their" and points back to 仕宦之家 "families of
officials". 其 stands **before** a noun (or a verb phrase), never after a verb: it is the
"his, her, its, their" pronoun. See [其: "his, her, its, their"](../../grammar/qi-pronoun.md).

**吾家 "our family".** 吾 can mean "I" or "we", "my" or "our". Context decides: here Zeng
is speaking for the whole family. See [Personal pronouns](../../grammar/personal-pronouns.md).

**此弊 "this fault".** 此 "this" stands before a noun, like English "this": 此弊 "this
fault", meaning the habit just described. See [Demonstratives](../../grammar/demonstratives.md).

**Parallel phrases.** 目前之快意甚少，將來之受累甚大 is built of two matching halves: "present
pleasure very small / future trouble very great". Parallel phrasing like this is a
constant feature of literary prose and a great help in reading it.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

In 輕棄**其**鄉, whose home district does 其 refer to?

- [ ] Zeng's son's
- [x] the families of officials'
- [ ] the mother's

**Explanation:** 其 points back to the subject of the sentence, 仕宦之家 "families of officials".

### 2. choice

What does 吾家 mean here?

- [ ] my house
- [x] our family
- [ ] I, the head of the family

**Explanation:** 吾 can be singular or plural; Zeng is speaking for the whole family.

### 3. short-answer

What does Zeng want the family to do, and why?

**Answer:** Return to their home village rather than stay in Hubei, because official families who cling to other provinces gain little pleasure now and suffer great trouble later.

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the sentence.

**Text:** 目前之快意甚少將來之受累甚大

**Answer:** 目前之快意甚少，將來之受累甚大。

**Explanation:** The two halves are parallel and are separated by a comma.

### 5. translate

Translate 吾家宜力矯此弊。

**Answer:** Our family must do its utmost to correct this fault.
