## Introduction

Family letters show how writers refer to themselves, to the person addressed, and to others.

## Objectives

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- identify personal pronouns and what they say about politeness
- follow 其 and 之 back to the person or thing they refer to
- use demonstratives 此, 彼, 是
