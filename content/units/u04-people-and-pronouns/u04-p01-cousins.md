## Introduction

The end of a letter from Zeng Guofan to his eldest son, Zeng Jize 曾紀澤 (1839–1890),
who later became a well-known diplomat. Letters from a father to a son are headed 諭
"instruction"; Zeng closes this one with 此諭 "these are my instructions". 臨三 and 昆八 are
two of the boy's cousins, called by family nicknames.

## Text

臨三、昆八，係親表兄弟，爾須與之互相勸勉。爾有所知者，常常與之講論，則彼此並進矣。此諭。

## Translation

Linsan and Kunba are your first cousins; you must encourage each other. Whatever you know, discuss it with them often, and then you will both make progress. These are my instructions.

## Literal translation

Linsan, Kunba, are first-cousins; you must with them mutually encourage. You have what-know 者, often with them discuss, then that-this together advance 矣. This instruction.

## Notes

**爾 "you".** Zeng addresses his son as 爾. 爾 and 汝 are used to someone younger or of
lower standing: a father to a son, an elder brother to a younger. To an equal or a superior
a writer avoids "you" altogether and uses a title or a polite term instead. See
[Personal pronouns](../../grammar/personal-pronouns.md).

**之 "them".** In 與之互相勸勉 and 與之講論, 之 is an object pronoun meaning "them",
referring back to the two cousins. 之 after a verb or preposition, with nothing following,
is a pronoun. See [之 as object pronoun](../../grammar/zhi-object.md).

**彼此 "each other".** 彼 "that (one)" and 此 "this (one)" together mean "both sides, each
other". On its own, 此 in 此諭 is "this". See [Demonstratives](../../grammar/demonstratives.md).

## Exercises

### 1. choice

In 爾須與**之**互相勸勉, what does 之 refer to?

- [ ] the son
- [x] the two cousins
- [ ] the father's instructions

**Explanation:** 之 after 與 "with" is an object pronoun, "them": Linsan and Kunba.

### 2. choice

Why does Zeng use 爾 "you" to his son?

- [x] 爾 is used by an elder to a younger person.
- [ ] 爾 is the polite word for "you".
- [ ] 爾 is used only in letters.

**Explanation:** 爾 and 汝 are used to someone younger or of lower standing. They would be impolite to an equal or a superior.

### 3. short-answer

What does Zeng say will happen if his son discusses what he knows with his cousins?

**Answer:** They will all make progress together (彼此並進矣).

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the sentence.

**Text:** 爾有所知者常常與之講論則彼此並進矣

**Answer:** 爾有所知者，常常與之講論，則彼此並進矣。

### 5. translate

Translate 臨三、昆八，係親表兄弟，爾須與之互相勸勉。

**Answer:** Linsan and Kunba are your first cousins; you must encourage each other.
