## Introduction

In the 1840s Zeng Guofan was an official in Beijing, and he wrote often to his younger
brothers at home about their studies. Here he advises his brother Zeng Guohua 曾國華
(溫弟) on calligraphy and recommends a friend to study with.

## Text

陳季牧最喜談字，且深思善悟。吾見其寄岱雲信，實能知寫字之法，可愛可畏，爾可從之切磋。此等好學之友，愈多愈好。

## Translation

Chen Jimu loves talking about calligraphy, and he thinks deeply and understands quickly. I have seen the letter he sent to Daiyun: he truly knows the method of writing characters. He is admirable and formidable, and you may go to him to polish your skills together. Of friends like this, who love learning, the more the better.

## Literal translation

Chen Jimu most likes talk characters, moreover deep-thinks quick-understands. I saw his send Daiyun letter, truly can know write-characters 之 method; lovable, fearsome; you may follow him polish. This-kind fond-of-learning 之 friends, the-more-many the-more-good.

## Notes

**One sentence, three pronouns.** 吾見其寄岱雲信 has 吾 "I" as subject and 其 "his" before
寄岱雲信 "sent to Daiyun a letter": "I saw his sending a letter to Daiyun", i.e. "I saw
the letter he sent to Daiyun". Later, 爾可從之切磋 has 爾 "you" and 之 "him".

**其 and 之: before or after.** Both can refer to the same person, but in different places:

| Pronoun | Position | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 其 | **before** a noun or verb phrase: "his", "he (who…)" | 其寄岱雲信 "the letter he sent to Daiyun" |
| 之 | **after** a verb or preposition: "him" | 從之 "follow him" |

See [其: "his, her, its, their"](../../grammar/qi-pronoun.md) and [之 as object pronoun](../../grammar/zhi-object.md).

**Three 之 in one passage.** 寫字之法 and 好學之友 join a modifier to a noun ("the method of
writing characters", "friends fond of learning"); 從之 is the pronoun. The test is the same
as before: does a noun follow 之, or nothing?

**Zeng to his brother.** Zeng uses 爾 "you" to his younger brother, as to his son: 爾 is the
"you" of an elder speaking to a younger relative.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

In 吾見**其**寄岱雲信, what does 其 mean?

- [ ] Daiyun's
- [x] Chen Jimu's: "the letter he sent to Daiyun"
- [ ] Zeng's own

**Explanation:** 其 refers back to the person being discussed, Chen Jimu.

### 2. choice

Which 之 is a pronoun?

- [ ] 寫字**之**法
- [x] 從**之**切磋
- [ ] 好學**之**友

**Explanation:** In 從之, 之 follows the verb 從 and stands for Chen Jimu: "go to him". In the other two, a noun follows 之 (法, 友).

### 3. short-answer

What two qualities of Chen Jimu does Zeng praise in the first sentence?

**Answer:** He thinks deeply (深思) and understands quickly (善悟).

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the sentence.

**Text:** 此等好學之友愈多愈好

**Answer:** 此等好學之友，愈多愈好。

### 5. translate

Translate 爾可從之切磋。
