## Introduction

The 中華民國臨時約法 (Provisional Constitution of the Republic of China) was passed by
the Provisional Senate in Nanjing and published on 11 March 1912, a few weeks after the
abdication of the last Qing emperor. Its articles are short and plainly written. These five
come from chapter 2, on the people. The original was printed without punctuation.

## Text

第五條：中華民國人民一律平等，無種族、階級、宗教之區別。

第七條：人民有請願於議會之權。

第十二條：人民有選舉及被選舉之權。

第十三條：人民依法律有納稅之義務。

第十四條：人民依法律有服兵之義務。

## Translation

Article 5: The people of the Republic of China are all equal, without distinction of race, class or religion.

Article 7: The people have the right to petition the assembly.

Article 12: The people have the right to elect and to be elected.

Article 13: The people have the duty to pay taxes according to the law.

Article 14: The people have the duty to do military service according to the law.

## Literal translation

Article-five: China-Republic people uniformly equal, have-no race, class, religion 之 distinction.

Article-seven: People have petition to assembly 之 right.

Article-twelve: People have elect and be-elected 之 right.

Article-thirteen: People according-to law have pay-tax 之 duty.

Article-fourteen: People according-to law have serve-army 之 duty.

## Notes

**有 and 無.** 有 "to have, there is" and 無 "to have no, there is no" are verbs, and their
object follows: 有…之權 "have the right…", 無…之區別 "have no distinction…". See
[有 and 無: existence](../../grammar/you-wu.md).

**Verb and object.** The basic order is the same as in English: verb, then object. Many
two-character words are themselves a verb with its object: 納稅 "pay + tax", 服兵 "serve (in) + army".

**A verb phrase describing a noun.** In 人民有請願於議會之權, the right (權) is described by a
whole verb phrase, 請願於議會 "to petition the assembly", joined to it by 之. English needs
"the right **to** petition". Verbs can work as modifiers just as nouns can: see
[Word classes and flexibility](../../grammar/word-classes.md).

**選舉 and 被選舉.** 被 before a verb makes it passive: 選舉 "to elect", 被選舉 "to be
elected". Passives are taught later, in [Passive constructions](../../grammar/passives.md).

## Exercises

### 1. choice

In 無種族、階級、宗教之區別, what does 無 mean?

- [ ] not
- [x] there is no; without
- [ ] not yet

**Explanation:** 無 is a verb, "to have no", with an object (…之區別): "(there is) no distinction of race, class or religion".

### 2. choice

What does **被**選舉 mean?

- [ ] to elect
- [x] to be elected
- [ ] to elect each other

**Explanation:** 被 before a verb makes it passive.

### 3. short-answer

Which of these five articles state duties rather than rights?

**Answer:** Articles 13 and 14 (納稅之義務 "the duty to pay taxes", 服兵之義務 "the duty to do military service").

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the article.

**Text:** 中華民國人民一律平等無種族階級宗教之區別

**Answer:** 中華民國人民一律平等，無種族、階級、宗教之區別。

**Explanation:** 、 separates the items of a list (種族、階級、宗教).

### 5. translate

Translate 人民依法律有納稅之義務。

**Answer:** The people have the duty to pay taxes according to the law.
