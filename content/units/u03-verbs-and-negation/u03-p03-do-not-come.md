## Introduction

Zeng Guofan 曾國藩 (1811–1872), scholar-official and commander against the Taiping,
wrote hundreds of letters to his family. The first paragraph comes from an early letter
to two of his younger brothers. Zeng recalls saying goodbye to one of his mother's
brothers before leaving for the capital. The second paragraph closes a letter of 1863 to
his brother Zeng Guoquan (沅弟), who had just received a reprimand from the court.

## Text

南五舅送至長沙，握手曰：「明年送外甥婦來京。」余曰：「京城苦，舅勿來。」舅曰：「然。然吾終尋汝任所也。」言已泣下。

余不能與弟相見，託黃南翁面語一切，冀弟毋動肝氣。至囑至囑。

## Translation

Fifth Uncle Nan saw me off as far as Changsha. Taking my hand, he said: "Next year I will bring your wife to the capital." I said: "Life in the capital is hard. Uncle, do not come." My uncle said: "Very well. But in the end I shall seek you out at your post." When he had finished speaking, he wept.

I cannot see you, so I have asked Mr Huang Nan to tell you everything in person. I hope that you will not lose your temper. I urge this most earnestly, most earnestly.

## Literal translation

Nan fifth-uncle saw-off to Changsha, took-hand said: "Next-year bring nephew's-wife come capital." I said: "Capital hard, uncle do-not come." Uncle said: "So. But I in-the-end seek-out your post 也." Speaking finished, wept.

I cannot with brother see-each-other, ask Huang Nan old-gentleman in-person tell everything, hope brother not stir liver-qi. Most-urge most-urge.

## Notes

**勿 and 毋 "do not".** 舅勿來 "uncle, do not come" is a direct prohibition. 毋 is used the
same way, and also after verbs of hoping or urging: 冀弟毋動肝氣 "(I) hope (you,) brother,
will not lose your temper". See [勿 and 毋: "do not"](../../grammar/prohibitives.md).

**不 is a plain "not".** 余不能與弟相見 "I cannot see you" simply negates the verb phrase 能…相見. It does not forbid anything.

**Speaking to family.** Zeng calls his brother 弟 "younger brother" where English would say
"you", and his uncle 舅 "uncle". Pronouns such as 余, 吾 and 汝 are taught in the next unit,
[People and pronouns](../u04-people-and-pronouns/unit.md).

**然 twice.** 然。然吾終尋汝任所也: the first 然 is "so (it is)", agreeing; the second is "but".
Context and position (the second begins a new sentence with a contrast) tell them apart.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

What does 舅**勿**來 mean?

- [ ] Uncle did not come.
- [x] Uncle, do not come.
- [ ] Uncle has not yet come.

**Explanation:** 勿 forbids: "do not". 不 would just say "did not / does not come"; 未 "has not yet come".

### 2. choice

Which sentence forbids or warns against something?

- [ ] 余不能與弟相見
- [x] 冀弟毋動肝氣
- [ ] 言已泣下

**Explanation:** 毋 "do not" after 冀 "hope": "I hope you will not lose your temper". 不能 only says "cannot".

### 3. short-answer

How does Uncle Nan answer Zeng's request?

**Answer:** He agrees (然 "very well"), but says that in the end he will seek Zeng out at his post anyway.

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate Zeng's reply.

**Text:** 京城苦舅勿來

**Answer:** 京城苦，舅勿來。

### 5. translate

Translate 冀弟毋動肝氣。

**Answer:** I hope that you (my brother) will not lose your temper.
