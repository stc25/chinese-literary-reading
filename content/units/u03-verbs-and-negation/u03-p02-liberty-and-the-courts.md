## Introduction

More articles of the 1912 Provisional Constitution: the opening of article 6 on civil
liberties, with its first two items (the remaining five are left out), and two articles
from later chapters, on judges and on the status of the Provisional Constitution itself.

## Text

第六條：人民得享有左列各項之自由權：

一、人民之身體，非依法律不得逮捕、拘禁、審問、處罰。

二、人民之家宅，非依法律不得侵入或搜索。……

第五十一條：法官獨立審判，不受上級官廳之干涉。

第五十四條：中華民國之憲法，由國會制定。憲法未施行以前，本約法之效力與憲法等。

## Translation

Article 6: The people shall enjoy the following civil liberties:

1. The person of the people may not be arrested, detained, interrogated or punished except according to law.

2. The homes of the people may not be entered or searched except according to law. …

Article 51: Judges try cases independently and are not subject to interference from higher government offices.

Article 54: The constitution of the Republic of China shall be drawn up by the parliament. Until the constitution has been put into effect, this Provisional Constitution has the same force as a constitution.

## Literal translation

Article-six: People may enjoy listed-left each-item 之 freedom-rights:

One, people 之 body, not according-to law may-not arrest, detain, interrogate, punish.

Two, people 之 home, not according-to law may-not enter or search. …

Article-fifty-one: Judges independently try, not subject-to higher government-office 之 interference.

Article-fifty-four: China-Republic 之 constitution, by parliament drawn-up. Constitution not-yet in-effect before, this provisional-constitution 之 force with constitution equal.

## Notes

**Four kinds of "not".**

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 不 | not (with verbs) | 不受上級官廳之干涉 "not subject to interference" |
| 不得 | may not | 不得逮捕 "may not arrest" |
| 未 | not yet | 憲法未施行 "the constitution has not yet been put into effect" |
| 非 | is not; unless | 非依法律 "(if it is) not according to law" |

See [不: negating verbs](../../grammar/bu-negation.md), [未: "not yet"](../../grammar/wei-not-yet.md)
and [非: "is not"](../../grammar/fei-negation.md).

**非…不得… "unless …, may not …".** 非依法律不得逮捕 is literally "not according to law, may
not arrest". Two negatives make a condition: arrest is allowed **only** according to law.

**No subject, and no passive.** 人民之身體 "the person of the people" is the topic, but it
is not the one arresting. Chinese does not need a passive here: the topic tells you what
the sentence is about, and the verbs (逮捕, 拘禁…) say what may not be done to it. English
uses a passive: "may not be arrested". See [Topic and comment](../../grammar/topic-comment.md).

**未…以前.** 憲法未施行以前 is literally "before the constitution has *not yet* been put
into effect". The 未 adds nothing in English: translate "until" or "before".

**左列 "the following".** The original was printed in vertical columns read from right to
left, so the list that follows stands to the **left**. You will meet vertical text in
[Reading the original page](../u11-the-original-page/unit.md).

## Exercises

### 1. choice

In 憲法**未**施行以前, what does 未 mean?

- [ ] is not
- [x] not yet
- [ ] there is no

**Explanation:** 未 negates something that has not (yet) happened.

### 2. choice

What does 非依法律不得逮捕 mean?

- [ ] The law does not allow arrest.
- [x] Arrest is not allowed except according to law.
- [ ] The people may not arrest anyone.

**Explanation:** 非…不得… "unless …, may not …": arrest is allowed only according to law.

### 3. choice

Why does the text say 左列 "listed on the left" for "the following"?

- [ ] Left is the side of honour.
- [x] The original was printed in vertical columns read from right to left.
- [ ] It refers to a separate table.

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the article.

**Text:** 法官獨立審判不受上級官廳之干涉

**Answer:** 法官獨立審判，不受上級官廳之干涉。

### 5. translate

Translate 憲法未施行以前，本約法之效力與憲法等。

**Answer:** Until the constitution has been put into effect, this Provisional Constitution has the same force as a constitution.
