## Introduction

Zeng Guofan's advice on study to his younger brothers, from two letters of the 1840s.
Paragraph 1 is about calligraphy; paragraphs 2 and 3 are about keeping up a daily reading
programme, whatever happens. His key word is 有恆 "perseverance".

## Text

習字臨《千字文》亦可，但須有恆。每日臨帖一百字，萬萬無間斷，則數年必成書家矣。

雖極忙，亦須了本日功課，不以昨日耽擱而今日補做，不以明日有事而今日預做。

切勿以家中有事而間斷看書之課，又弗以考試將近而間斷看書之課。雖走路之日，到店亦可看；考試之日，出場亦可看也。

## Translation

For practising calligraphy, copying the Thousand Character Classic is fine too, but you must persevere. Copy a hundred characters from the model every day, absolutely without a break, and in a few years you will certainly have become a calligrapher.

Even when you are extremely busy, you must still finish each day's lessons on that day. Do not, because of a delay yesterday, make the work up today; and do not, because you will be busy tomorrow, do the work today in advance.

On no account interrupt your daily reading because there is business at home, nor interrupt it because the examinations are approaching. Even on a travelling day you can read once you reach the inn; on an examination day you can read once you come out of the hall.

## Notes

**Modal verbs.** Words of possibility and obligation stand directly before the verb:

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 可 | may, can; (it) is acceptable | 到店亦可看 "you can read once you reach the inn" |
| 須 | must | 但須有恆 "but you must persevere" |
| 必 | certainly, will surely | 數年必成書家 "in a few years you will surely become a calligrapher" |

You have already met 能 "can" (不能 "cannot"), 得 "may" (不得 "may not") and 宜 "should" (吾家宜力矯此弊). See [Modal verbs](../../grammar/modal-verbs.md).

**亦可 "is also fine".** 可 can stand alone at the end of a clause: 習字臨《千字文》亦可
"(for) practising calligraphy, copying the Thousand Character Classic is also acceptable".

**…則…必…矣.** 每日臨帖一百字…，則數年必成書家矣 "(if you) copy a hundred characters
every day…, then in a few years you will surely have become a calligrapher". 則 introduces
the result, 必 says it is certain, and 矣 presents it as the new state that will have come about.

**將近 "approaching".** 將 marks what is about to happen: 考試將近 "the examinations are
drawing near". See [Aspect](../../grammar/aspect-markers.md).

**了 is read liǎo.** In 了本日功課, 了 is a verb, "to finish". In later vernacular texts the
same character, read le, marks a completed action; see [Vernacular elements](../../grammar/vernacular-elements.md).

What does 須 mean in 但**須**有恆?

- [x] must

### 2. choice

In 數年**必**成書家矣, what does 必 express?

- [ ] permission
- [ ] obligation
- [x] certainty: "will surely"

### 3. choice

What does 考試**將**近 mean?

- [ ] The examinations are over.
- [x] The examinations are approaching.
- [ ] The examinations will be held nearby.

**Explanation:** 將 marks what is about to happen.

### 4. short-answer

According to Zeng, when can his brothers read on a travelling day and on an examination day?

**Answer:** On a travelling day, once they reach the inn; on an examination day, once they come out of the examination hall.

### 5. punctuate

Punctuate the sentence.

**Text:** 習字臨千字文亦可但須有恆

**Answer:** 習字臨《千字文》亦可，但須有恆。

**Explanation:** 《》 marks the book title; 但 "but" begins a new clause.
