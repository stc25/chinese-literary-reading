## Introduction

In 1889 Xue Fucheng 薛福成 was appointed envoy to Britain, France, Italy and Belgium. He kept a
diary of his mission, later published as 出使英法義比四國日記. The first entry, written on
board ship in early 1890, looks back over the months before his departure. Here he records
the death of his eldest brother in 1889 (光緒十五年). The dates are months and days of the
Chinese calendar.

## Text

伯兄撫屏，因中風不語，已奏請開副都御史缺，亦於五月杪出都，六月十五日抵無錫。福成於二十八日回錫，則伯兄病已革矣；七月初二日，遂至不起。

## Translation

My eldest brother Fuping had suffered a stroke and could not speak, and he had already petitioned the throne to be relieved of his post as vice censor-in-chief. He too left the capital, at the end of the fifth month, and reached Wuxi on the fifteenth day of the sixth month. When I returned to Wuxi on the twenty-eighth, my brother's illness had become critical; on the second day of the seventh month he died.

## Notes

**Time without tenses.** Chinese verbs have no tenses. The order of events is shown by
dates (五月杪, 六月十五日…), by the order of the clauses, and by a few words that mark how an
action relates to time:

- **已 "already"**: 已奏請 "had already petitioned"; 病已革 "the illness had already become critical";
- **遂 "thereupon, then"**: 遂至不起 "and then he died", one event following from another.

See [Aspect: 已, 既, 嘗, 將, 方](../../grammar/aspect-markers.md).

**矣 "it has (now) become…".** 伯兄病已革矣 "my brother's illness had become critical".
矣 at the end of a clause marks a **change**: something is now different from before. See
[矣: a new situation](../../grammar/yi-final.md).

**則 "then (I found that)".** 福成於二十八日回錫，則伯兄病已革矣: "when I returned…,
(I found that) my brother's illness had become critical". 則 introduces what the writer
found on arrival.

**Speaking of oneself.** Xue calls himself 福成, his personal name, rather than 余 or 吾.
Officials did this in writing meant to be read by superiors or published; it is modest
and formal.

**不起 "not rise".** "Not to rise again" is a polite way of saying that someone died.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

What does 已 add in 伯兄病**已**革矣?

- [ ] It makes the sentence negative.
- [x] It says the illness had already become critical by the time Xue arrived.
- [ ] It means "because".

**Explanation:** 已 marks an action or state as already in place at the time referred to.

### 2. choice

What does 矣 do at the end of 伯兄病已革矣?

- [ ] It asks a question.
- [ ] It marks a definition.
- [x] It marks a change: the illness had now become critical.

**Explanation:** 矣 marks a new situation, different from before.

### 3. choice

What does 遂至不起 mean?

- [ ] He was then unable to get up.
- [x] He then died.
- [ ] He finally recovered.

**Explanation:** 不起 "not rise again" is a polite way of saying "die"; 遂 "thereupon" links it to the illness before.
