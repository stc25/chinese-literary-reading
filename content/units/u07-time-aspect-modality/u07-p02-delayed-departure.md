## Introduction

Two more sentences from the same diary entry. Xue twice tried to leave for Europe in late
1889: first illness stopped him, then a telegram warning of an epidemic. This was the
influenza pandemic that swept through Europe in the winter of 1889–90. The text between
the two sentences, on his treatment and recovery, is left out.

## Text

方擬十月初旬出洋，而瘧疾遽發。

方擬於十二月十四日乘坐法公司「揚子」輪船啟行，已購船票矣，忽接前任出使大臣劉芝田中丞來電云：德法時疫正盛，英亦傳染，望明正起程為妥。

## Translation

Just as I was planning to go abroad in the first ten days of the tenth month, I suddenly came down with malaria.

Just as I was planning to set off on the fourteenth day of the twelfth month on the French company's steamer Yangzi, and had already bought the tickets, I suddenly received a telegram from my predecessor as envoy, Governor Liu Zhitian, saying: "The epidemic is at its height in Germany and France, and Britain too is infected. I hope you will set out in the first month of next year; that would be best."

## Notes

**方 "just as, just then".** Both sentences begin 方擬… "just as I was planning…". 方 says
that something was in progress, or about to happen, when something else intervened: 而瘧疾遽發
"but malaria suddenly broke out"; 忽接…來電 "suddenly I received a telegram". The pair 方…忽…
"just as … suddenly …" is a common way of telling a story.

**已…矣 "had already…".** 已購船票矣 "(I) had already bought the tickets". 已 marks what
was already done; 矣 confirms the completed change. Together they are common in narrative.
See [矣: a new situation](../../grammar/yi-final.md).

**正 "just now, at its height".** In 時疫正盛 "the epidemic is (just now) at its height",
正 marks a state in progress. In 明正 "the first month of next year", the same character is
read zhēng and means "first month" (正月). See [Aspect](../../grammar/aspect-markers.md).

**Telegrams.** By 1890 China's officials were linked to Europe by telegraph. 來電 "an
incoming telegram" and 電奏 "to report to the throne by telegram" are new terms of the period.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

What does **方**擬…，而瘧疾遽發 mean?

- [ ] Because I was planning…, malaria broke out.
- [x] Just as I was planning…, malaria suddenly broke out.
- [ ] After I had planned…, malaria broke out.

**Explanation:** 方 "just as" marks an action in progress that is interrupted.

### 2. choice

In 已購船票矣, what do 已 and 矣 tell you together?

- [x] The tickets had already been bought.
- [ ] The tickets would be bought soon.
- [ ] The tickets could not be bought.

### 3. short-answer

Why did Liu Zhitian advise Xue to delay his departure?

**Answer:** Because an epidemic was at its height in Germany and France, and Britain too was infected.

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the telegram.

**Text:** 德法時疫正盛英亦傳染望明正起程為妥

**Answer:** 德法時疫正盛，英亦傳染，望明正起程為妥。

### 5. translate

Translate 方擬十月初旬出洋，而瘧疾遽發。
