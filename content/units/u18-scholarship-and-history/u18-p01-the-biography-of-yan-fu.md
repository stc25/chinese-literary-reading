## Introduction

When the Qing fell, the Republic did what every new dynasty had done: it set up an office to
write the history of the last one. The 清史稿 "Draft History of the Qing" (1927–28) follows the
pattern of the official histories since Sima Qian: annals, treatises, tables, and above all
biographies (列傳). The compilers were Qing loyalists, and their judgements show it.

This is the biography of Yan Fu, whose translations you read in [U17](../u17-thought-and-translation/unit.md).
It is appended (附) to the biography of the translator Lin Shu, in the section on men of letters
(文苑傳). Notice the fixed order: names, native place, early life, career by dates, opinions,
writings quoted, death, works. Notice too how the biographer quotes: 其……有曰：「……」.

## Text

嚴復，初名宗光，字又陵，一字幾道，侯官人。早慧，嗜為文。閩督沈葆楨初創船政，招試英俊，儲海軍將才，得復文，奇之，用冠其曹，則年十四也。既卒業，從軍艦練習，周歷南洋、黃海。……光緒二年，派赴英國海軍學校肄戰術及砲台建築諸學，每試輒最。侍郎郭嵩燾使英，賞其才，時引與論析中西學術同異。學成歸，北洋大臣李鴻章方大治海軍，以復總學堂。二十四年，詔求人才，復被薦，召對稱旨。諭繕所擬萬言書以進，未及用，而政局猝變。越二年，避拳亂南歸。

是時人士漸傾向西人學說，復以為自由、平等、權利諸說，由之未嘗無利，脫靡所折衷，則流蕩放佚，害且不可勝言，常於廣眾中陳之。……復殫心著述，於學無所不窺，舉中外治術學理，靡不究極原委，抉其失得，證明而會通之。精歐西文字，所譯書以朅辭達奧旨。

其《天演論・自序》有曰：「仲尼之於六藝也，《易》、《春秋》最嚴。司馬遷曰：『《易》本隱而之顯，《春秋》推見至隱。』此天下至精之言也。始吾以為本隱之顯者，觀像繫辭，以定吉凶而已；推見至隱者，誅意褒貶而已。及觀西人名學，則見其格物致知之事，有內籀之術焉，有外籀之術焉。內籀云者，察其曲而知其全者也，執其微以會其通者也。外籀云者，援公理以斷眾事者也，設定數以逆未然者也。是固吾《易》、《春秋》之學也。遷所謂『本隱之顯』者外籀也，所謂『推見至隱』者內籀也，二者即物窮理之要術也。……」凡復所譯著，獨得精微皆類此。

世謂紓以中文溝通西文，復以西文溝通中文，並稱「林嚴」。辛酉秋，卒，年六十有九。著有《文集》及譯《天演論》、《原富》、《群學肄言》、《穆勒名學》、《法意》、《群己權界論》、《社會通詮》等。

## Translation

Yan Fu, originally named Zongguang, courtesy name Youling, another courtesy name Jidao, was a native of Houguan. He was precocious and loved writing. When Shen Baozhen, governor-general of Fujian, first founded the Navy Yard, he recruited and examined talented youths to train them as naval officers. He read Yan Fu's essay, thought it remarkable, and placed him first of his group; Yan was then fourteen. After completing his studies he trained on warships and travelled all over the South Seas and the Yellow Sea. …… In the 2nd year of Guangxu he was sent to the naval college in England to study tactics, the construction of fortifications and other subjects, and came first in every examination. Vice-President Guo Songtao, envoy to Britain, admired his talent and often invited him to discuss the similarities and differences between Chinese and Western learning. When he returned from his studies, Li Hongzhang, Commissioner for the Northern Ports, was building up the navy on a large scale, and made him head of the academy. In the 24th year an edict called for men of talent; Yan was recommended, summoned to an audience, and pleased the emperor. He was ordered to write out his memorial of ten thousand words and submit it, but before it could be used the political situation suddenly changed. Two years later he fled the Boxer disturbances and went south.

At that time people were increasingly inclined towards Western theories. Yan held that theories of freedom, equality and rights were not without benefit if followed, but that without moderation they would run wild and unrestrained, and the harm would be beyond words; he often said so before large gatherings. …… Yan devoted himself wholly to writing. There was no field of learning he did not look into; taking up the methods of government and principles of learning of China and abroad, there was none he did not trace from beginning to end, picking out their faults and merits, proving them and bringing them together. He was expert in European languages, and his translations convey profound meaning in splendid language.

In his preface to *On Evolution* he says: "Of the Six Classics, Confucius was most rigorous in the *Changes* and the *Spring and Autumn Annals*. Sima Qian said: 'The *Changes* starts from the hidden and goes to the manifest; the *Spring and Autumn* infers from the manifest to reach the hidden.' This is the most penetrating saying in the world. At first I thought that starting from the hidden and going to the manifest meant only observing the images and attaching the judgements to determine good and bad fortune, and that inferring from the manifest to reach the hidden meant only condemning intentions and assigning praise and blame. But when I looked at Western logic, I saw that in the investigation of things and extension of knowledge there is a method of induction and a method of deduction. What is called induction is examining the particulars and knowing the whole, grasping the minute and reaching the general. What is called deduction is applying general laws to judge particular cases, positing fixed quantities to predict what has not yet happened. This is in fact the learning of our *Changes* and *Spring and Autumn*. What Sima Qian called 'starting from the hidden and going to the manifest' is deduction; what he called 'inferring from the manifest to reach the hidden' is induction; these two are the essential methods for approaching things and exhausting their principles. ……" In all his translations and writings, Yan's unique grasp of subtle points was like this.

The world said that Lin Shu connected Western writing through Chinese and Yan Fu connected Chinese writing through Western, and named them together as "Lin and Yan". He died in the autumn of the xinyou year, aged sixty-nine. His works include his *Collected Writings*, and the translations *On Evolution*, *The Wealth of Nations*, *The Study of Sociology*, *Mill's Logic*, *The Spirit of the Laws*, *On the Boundary between Group and Self*, and *A Comprehensive Account of Society*.

## Notes

**The order of a biography.** Official biographies follow a fixed sequence:

| Part | Here |
|---|---|
| names | 嚴復，初名宗光，字又陵，一字幾道 |
| native place (籍貫) | 侯官人 |
| early life and character | 早慧，嗜為文 |
| career, dated by reign year | 光緒二年……二十四年……越二年…… |
| opinions and achievements | 是時人士漸傾向西人學說，復以為…… |
| a quotation from his writings | 其《天演論・自序》有曰：「……」 |
| reputation | 世謂…… |
| death and age | 辛酉秋，卒，年六十有九 |
| works | 著有…… |

After the first mention, the subject is called by his given name alone: 復, 紓. See
[Conventions of biography](../../grammar/biography-conventions.md).

**Dates.** The biographer dates by Qing reign years (光緒二年, 二十四年) while Yan served the
Qing, but by the cyclical year alone for his death (辛酉, 1921): a loyalist history will not
use the Republic's 民國十年. See [Dates and calendars](../../toolkit/t02-dates-and-calendars.md).

**Quotations within quotations.** The quotation from Yan's preface itself quotes Sima Qian:
「……司馬遷曰：『……』……」. Then Yan picks up Sima Qian's phrases again with 遷所謂『……』者.
所謂 "what is called" is how a scholar refers back to a phrase already quoted. See
[Commentary and citation](../../grammar/citation-conventions.md).

**Induction and deduction.** Yan's 內籀 "inward drawing-out" for induction and 外籀 "outward
drawing-out" for deduction did not last (modern 歸納 and 演繹). The passage shows his method:
he finds Western logic already in the *Changes* and the *Spring and Autumn*. The biographer
admires this; it is exactly the kind of claim that later scholars questioned.

**Facts to check.** A biography in an official history is a judgement, not a neutral record. This
one stresses Yan's warning against "freedom, equality and rights" and pairs him with the loyalist
Lin Shu. It says nothing of his support for Yuan Shikai's monarchy in 1915. Yan in fact sailed
for England in 1877, not 1876 as the reign date suggests, and studied at the Royal Naval College,
Greenwich.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

What is 又陵?

- [ ] Yan Fu's original given name
- [x] Yan Fu's courtesy name
- [ ] Yan Fu's native place

### 2. short-answer

How does the biographer date Yan Fu's death, and why not by the Republican calendar?

**Answer:** By the cyclical year 辛酉 (1921). The 清史稿 was written by Qing loyalists, who avoided the Republic's reign title.

### 3. short-answer

What did Yan Fu think of the theories of freedom, equality and rights?

**Answer:** They were not without benefit, but without moderation (脫靡所折衷) they would run wild and do great harm.

### 4. translate

Translate 內籀云者，察其曲而知其全者也。

**Answer:** What is called induction is examining the particulars and knowing the whole.

Punctuate this sentence.

**Text:** 世謂紓以中文溝通西文復以西文溝通中文並稱林嚴辛酉秋卒年六十有九
