## Introduction

Every Chinese county, prefecture and province had its gazetteer (方志, 地方志): a compilation of
its geography, institutions, taxes, schools, notable people, monuments and literature, revised
every few generations. For historians they are a basic source on local society. They are also
compilations of compilations: each entry is copied from earlier gazetteers and books, with the
new compiler's comments added.

This passage comes from the new gazetteer of Hechuan 合川 (formerly 合州) in Sichuan, edited in
1921 by the historian Zhang Senkai. Above the town stands 釣魚山 "Fishing Mountain", where the
fortress of 釣魚城 held out against the Mongols for over thirty years, and where the Great Khan
Möngke (元憲宗) died during the siege in 1259. Read four entries from the section on historic
sites (古蹟), and notice how each ends by naming its source and who added the comment.

## Text

釣魚山在州東隔江五里。《輿地紀勝》云：「涪內水在其南，西漢上流經其北。山南大石砥平，有巨人跡。相傳異人坐其上，投釣江中，因以名山。今插竿之目猶存。」任逢詩云：「雖無渭水周家望，合有嚴陵漢室光。」詠其事也。然予兩度尋之，初不得其處；問之故老，亦無有能指其地者也。張乃孚《合州志》，森楷坿記。

石子山在州下流十五里，元憲宗自雞爪灘渡江上岸處卽此。森楷自記。

龜山在縣下流六里南岸，與釣魚山對峙，元憲宗親征合州駐蹕之所。今名雨台山，又以憲宗中礮風處，一名礮台山。張乃孚《合州志》，森楷坿記。

牟山，一名西台山，在州西十里。《通志》作二十里，誤，今删二字。唐康元良（《通志》作元朗）讀晝處，有讀書臺存。案：康元良不知何許人，其臺今亦不知所在。張乃孚《合州志》，森楷坿記。

## Translation

Fishing Mountain is five li east of the town, across the river. The *Records of Famous Places* says: "The inner Fu river runs to its south, and the upper course of the Xihan passes to its north. South of the mountain is a great rock, flat as a whetstone, with a giant's footprint. It is said that an immortal sat on it and cast his line into the river, and so the mountain was named after this. The hole where he planted his rod still exists." Ren Feng's poem says: "Though it has no Wang of the Zhou house by the Wei river, it should have Yan Ling's glory for the house of Han": this celebrates the story. But I looked for it twice and could not find the place at all; I asked the old people, and none of them could point out the spot either. From Zhang Naifu's *Gazetteer of Hezhou*, with an appended note by Senkai.

Stone Mountain is fifteen li downstream from the town. The place where Xianzong of the Yuan crossed the river from Chicken-Claw Rapids and landed is here. Note by Senkai.

Turtle Mountain is on the south bank six li downstream from the town, facing Fishing Mountain. It is where Xianzong of the Yuan halted when he led the campaign against Hezhou in person. It is now called Yutai Mountain; and because it was where Xianzong was struck by the blast of a cannon, it is also called Cannon Terrace Mountain. From Zhang Naifu's *Gazetteer of Hezhou*, with an appended note by Senkai.

Mou Mountain, also called West Terrace Mountain, is ten li west of the town. The *Provincial Gazetteer* has twenty li, which is wrong; we now delete the two characters. At the place where Kang Yuanliang of the Tang (the *Provincial Gazetteer* has Yuanlang) studied, there is a reading terrace. Note: it is not known who Kang Yuanliang was, and the location of his terrace is now also unknown. From Zhang Naifu's *Gazetteer of Hezhou*, with an appended note by Senkai.

## Notes

**Layers of text.** A gazetteer entry is built in layers, and the last words tell you whose
layer is whose:

| Formula | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 張乃孚《合州志》 | the entry is copied from Zhang Naifu's earlier gazetteer of Hezhou (Qing) |
| 森楷坿記 | Zhang Senkai has added a note to it |
| 森楷自記 | the entry is Zhang Senkai's own |
| 《輿地紀勝》云 | a quotation from an older book, inside the entry |
| 案 | the compiler's comment begins |

In 釣魚山 there are three layers: the Song *Records of Famous Places*, Zhang Naifu's gazetteer,
which quotes it and Ren Feng's poem, and Zhang Senkai's note. Who is the 予 "I" who looked for the
footprint? Probably the last compiler, but the text does not say.

**Where does a quotation end?** The original has no quotation marks, only 。 after every clause
(compare the original text). 云 "says" opens a quotation, but nothing closes it.
Our 「」 end the *Records* at 猶存, because 任逢詩云 begins a new citation and 詠其事也 is a comment
on it. Another editor might punctuate differently. When you use a gazetteer, decide where each
quotation ends before you cite it.

**Correcting the sources.** In 牟山 the compiler checks the provincial gazetteer (《通志》) and
corrects it: 《通志》作二十里，誤，今删二字 "the *Provincial Gazetteer* has twenty li, which is
wrong; we now delete the two characters". 作 "has, reads" introduces a variant reading; (《通志》作
元朗) records another without deciding. This is the vocabulary of textual criticism: 作 "reads",
誤 "wrong", 删 "delete", 案 "note". See [Commentary and citation](../../grammar/citation-conventions.md).

**The death of Möngke.** 石子山 and 龜山 locate the Great Khan's campaign of 1259: where he landed,
where he camped, and where, according to local tradition, he was struck by a cannon shot from
the fortress. The official histories say only that he died at the siege. A gazetteer records
local memory that a national history leaves out.

**Allusions in Ren Feng's poem.** 渭水周家望: Lü Shang, the 太公望, was fishing on the Wei river
when King Wen of Zhou found him and made him his minister. 嚴陵漢室光: Yan Guang 嚴光 (Yan Ziling
嚴子陵) fished at Fuchun and refused office under the Han emperor Guangwu. The mountain may not
have produced a statesman, says the poet, but it should have the glory of a noble recluse.

**Distances.** 里 li in gazetteers are traditional li, roughly half a kilometre. 下流 "downstream"
and 隔江 "across the river" locate places from the town by the river, as people travelled. See
[Money, weights and measures](../../toolkit/t04-money-weights-measures.md).

### 1. choice

What does 森楷坿記 at the end of an entry mean?

- [ ] Zhang Senkai wrote the whole entry.

### 2. short-answer

What mistake in the provincial gazetteer does the 牟山 entry correct, and how?

**Answer:** The *Provincial Gazetteer* gives the distance as twenty li; the compiler says it is ten li and deletes the two characters (二十里 → 十里).

### 3. short-answer

Why are the quotation marks in our text a matter of judgement?

**Answer:** The original marks only pauses (。) and has no quotation marks. 云 opens a quotation but nothing marks where it ends, so the editor must decide.

### 4. translate

Translate 相傳異人坐其上，投釣江中，因以名山。

**Answer:** It is said that an immortal sat on it and cast his line into the river, and so the mountain was named after this.
