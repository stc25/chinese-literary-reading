## Introduction

You have read the opening articles of the Provisional Constitution of 1912 and its articles
on the people's rights (see [Verbs, objects and negation](../u03-verbs-and-negation/unit.md) and
[Reading the original page](../u11-the-original-page/unit.md)). This passage takes the article
that limits those rights and the articles that define the powers of the Provisional President.

The constitution was drafted in a hurry in February and March 1912, after the revolutionaries
had agreed that Yuan Shikai would replace Sun Yat-sen. Its authors wanted to bind the new
president to the Senate. Watch how the drafting does it: what the president **may** do (得),
and the conditions attached (但, 須, 經…之同意).

## Text

第十五條：本章所載人民之權利，有認爲增進公益、維持治安或非常緊急必要時，得依法律限制之。

第三十條：臨時大總統代表臨時政府，總攬政務，公布法律。

第三十一條：臨時大總統爲執行法律或基於法律之委任，得發布命令，並得使發布之。

第三十三條：臨時大總統得制定官制、官規，但須提交參議院議决。

第三十四條：臨時大總統任免文武職員。但任命國務員及外交大使、公使，須得參議院之同意。

第三十五條：臨時大總統經參議院之同意，得宣戰、媾和及締結條約。

第三十六條：臨時大總統得依法律宣告戒嚴。

第四十條：臨時大總統得宣告大赦、特赦、减刑、復權。但大赦須經參議院之同意。

## Translation

Article 15: The rights of the people set out in this chapter may be restricted according to law when this is deemed necessary to promote the public good or maintain public order, or in an extraordinary emergency.

Article 30: The Provisional President represents the Provisional Government, has overall charge of government affairs and promulgates the laws.

Article 31: The Provisional President may, in order to execute the laws or on the basis of powers delegated by law, issue orders, and may have them issued.

Article 33: The Provisional President may lay down the organisation and regulations of offices, but they must be submitted to the Senate for resolution.

Article 34: The Provisional President appoints and dismisses civil and military officials. But the appointment of members of the cabinet, ambassadors and ministers must have the consent of the Senate.

Article 35: The Provisional President may, with the consent of the Senate, declare war, make peace and conclude treaties.

Article 36: The Provisional President may proclaim martial law according to law.

Article 40: The Provisional President may proclaim general amnesties, special pardons, commutations of sentence and restorations of rights. But a general amnesty must have the consent of the Senate.

## Notes

**得 "may".** In legal language 得 dé before a verb grants a power or a permission: 得發布命令
"may issue orders". Its negative 不得 "may not" forbids (you met it in the treaties and in
article 6, 非依法律不得逮捕). Do not read it as "to get": in 須得參議院之同意 "must obtain the
Senate's consent", 得 is a full verb.

**Granting and limiting.** Most articles here grant a power and then limit it:

| Power | Limit | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| 得制定官制、官規 | 但須提交參議院議决 | grant, then 但 "provided that" |
| 任免文武職員 | 但任命國務員…須得參議院之同意 | grant, then 但 with an exception |
| 得宣戰、媾和及締結條約 | 經參議院之同意 | the condition placed **before** the power |
| 得宣告戒嚴 | 依法律 | "according to law" |

但 at the start of a second sentence in a law is a **proviso** (但書 dànshū): it introduces an
exception or a condition, and lawyers argue over it. See [Legal drafting](../../grammar/legal-language.md).

**Article 15: a limit on rights.** 有認爲…必要時，得依法律限制之 "when it is deemed necessary
…, (they) may be restricted by law". The subject is missing: who deems it necessary? The
article does not say. Legal Chinese often leaves out the agent, and the omission can matter.

**Lists.** Powers are listed with 、 and joined to the last item with 及: 宣戰、媾和及締結條約.
及 "and" is the legal word for joining the last item of a list.

**Who wrote it, and why.** Under Yuan Shikai, the Senate used these articles to block his
appointments; in 1914 he replaced the whole constitution with one that gave the president
almost unlimited power (中華民國約法). Reading the provisos (但, 須) is how you see what the authors feared.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

What does 得 mean in 臨時大總統得宣告戒嚴?

- [ ] to get, to obtain
- [x] may, is empowered to
- [ ] must

### 2. choice

What does 但 introduce in article 34?

- [ ] a new power of the president
- [x] an exception: certain appointments need the Senate's consent
- [ ] the date the article takes effect

### 3. short-answer

Which three powers of the president in article 35 need the Senate's consent?

**Answer:** Declaring war, making peace and concluding treaties (宣戰、媾和及締結條約).

### 4. short-answer

In article 15, who decides that restricting the people's rights is necessary?

**Answer:** The article does not say: the agent of 認爲 is left out. Only the means (依法律, by law) is specified.

### 5. punctuate

Punctuate article 40.

**Text:** 臨時大總統得宣告大赦特赦减刑復權但大赦須經參議院之同意

**Answer:** 臨時大總統得宣告大赦、特赦、减刑、復權。但大赦須經參議院之同意。
