## Introduction

The Sino-Japanese War of 1894–95 ended in a crushing defeat for China. The peace treaty,
signed at Shimonoseki (馬關, 下關) on 17 April 1895 by Li Hongzhang and Itō Hirobumi, ended
China's centuries-old relationship with Korea as a tributary state, ceded Taiwan, the Penghu
islands and the Liaodong peninsula to Japan, and imposed a huge indemnity.

The treaty was written in Chinese and Japanese, and an English text was added to settle
disputes between them. This passage gives the article on Korea, the article on the people of
the ceded territories, and the separate article on the English text.

## Text

第一款：中國認明朝鮮國確為完全無缺之獨立自主。故凡有虧損獨立自主體制，即如該國向中國所修貢獻典禮等，嗣後全行廢絶。

第五款：本約批准互換之後限二年之內，日本准中國讓與地方人民願遷居讓與地方之外者，任便變𧶠所有產業，退去界外。但限滿之後尚未遷徙者，酌宜視為日本臣民。

又臺灣一省應於本約批准互換後，兩國立即各派大員至臺灣，限於本約批准互換後兩箇月內交接清楚。

議定專條第一：彼此約明，本日署名蓋印之和約添備英文，與該約漢正文、日本正文較對無訛。

第二：彼此約明，日後設有兩國各執漢正文或日本正文有所辯論，即以上開英文約本為憑，以免舛錯，而昭公允。

## Translation

Article 1: China clearly recognises that Korea is definitely a fully and completely independent and autonomous state. Therefore all that impairs its independent and autonomous status, such as the tribute and ceremonies that country has performed to China, shall be entirely abolished from now on.

Article 5: Within two years of the exchange of ratifications of this treaty, Japan permits those inhabitants of the territories ceded by China who wish to move their residence outside the ceded territories to sell their property freely and withdraw beyond the boundary. But those who have not yet moved when the time limit expires shall, as is deemed appropriate, be regarded as Japanese subjects.

Further, as to the province of Taiwan, the two countries shall, immediately after the exchange of ratifications of this treaty, each send high officials to Taiwan, and complete the transfer within two months of the exchange of ratifications.

Separate article, item 1: It is mutually agreed that the peace treaty signed and sealed this day is provided additionally with an English text, which has been checked against the authentic Chinese and Japanese texts of the treaty and found to agree.

Item 2: It is mutually agreed that if in future there should be a dispute in which the two countries each hold to the authentic Chinese text or the authentic Japanese text, the above-mentioned English text of the treaty shall be the authority, so as to avoid errors and show fairness.

## Notes

**Three texts.** The treaty existed in Chinese, Japanese and English. The separate article
makes the English text the referee: neither side's language would prevail over the other's.
For a historian this means the Chinese text is not the last word, and the differences matter.

**Comparing the English.** The English text of article 1 reads, in substance: "China
recognises definitively the full and complete independence and autonomy of Corea, and, in
consequence, the payment of tribute and the performance of ceremonies and formalities by Corea
to China, in derogation of such independence and autonomy, shall wholly cease for the future."

| Chinese | English | |
|---|---|---|
| 認明 | recognises definitively | 明 "clearly" ↔ "definitively" |
| 完全無缺之獨立自主 | full and complete independence and autonomy | a four-character pair for each English pair |
| 凡有虧損獨立自主體制 | in derogation of such independence and autonomy | a clause in Chinese, a phrase in English |
| 全行廢絶 | shall wholly cease | |

In article 5, 酌宜視為日本臣民 "shall, as is deemed appropriate, be regarded as Japanese
subjects" corresponds to "shall, at the option of Japan, be deemed to be Japanese subjects".
The English says who decides (Japan); the Chinese 酌宜 leaves it vague. Such gaps are where
disputes start, and why treaties named an authoritative text.

**Treaty vocabulary.** 款 "article", 本約 "this treaty", 批准互換 "exchange of ratifications",
署名蓋印 "signed and sealed", 彼此約明 "it is mutually agreed", 正文 "authentic text",
為憑 "to be the authority". See [Treaty language](../../grammar/treaty-language.md).

**Time limits.** Legal and treaty drafting counts time from a fixed act: 本約批准互換之後限二年之內
"within two years after the exchange of ratifications". 限 is both the verb "to limit (to)"
and the noun "time limit" (限滿).

**Korea.** 朝鮮 had sent tribute to the Qing court; article 1 ends that relationship. The
"independence" of Korea opened the way to Japanese control and annexation in 1910.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

According to the separate article, what happens if the Chinese and Japanese texts are disputed?

- [ ] The Chinese text prevails.
- [ ] The Japanese text prevails.
- [x] The English text is the authority.

### 2. short-answer

What may the inhabitants of the ceded territories do within two years, and what happens to those who stay?

**Answer:** They may sell their property and move outside the ceded territories. Those who have not moved by the end of the two years may be regarded as Japanese subjects.

### 3. short-answer

The English text says "at the option of Japan" where the Chinese says 酌宜. What difference does this make?

**Answer:** The English makes clear that Japan decides; the Chinese 酌宜 "as is deemed appropriate" does not say who decides.

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate article 1.

**Text:** 中國認明朝鮮國確為完全無缺之獨立自主故凡有虧損獨立自主體制即如該國向中國所修貢獻典禮等嗣後全行廢絶
