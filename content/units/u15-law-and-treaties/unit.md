## Introduction

Laws and treaties use a precise, formulaic language of conditions, permissions and obligations. This unit reads the articles of the 1912 Provisional Constitution that define the president's powers, and two treaties that say which language decides a dispute: the Treaty of Tianjin (1858), where English prevails, and the Treaty of Shimonoseki (1895), where an English text referees between Chinese and Japanese. For a historian, the Chinese text of a treaty is one version among others, and the differences matter.

## Objectives

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- read legal drafting patterns
- follow the structure of a treaty article
- compare a Chinese treaty text with its other-language version
