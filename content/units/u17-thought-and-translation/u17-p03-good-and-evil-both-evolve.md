## Introduction

Zhang Binglin, also known as Zhang Taiyan, was a revolutionary, a classical philologist and a
student of Buddhist philosophy. He wrote in a deliberately archaic style, with rare characters
and old forms, and he is hard to read even for Chinese scholars. This essay, first published in
the revolutionary journal 《民報》 in 1906, is one of his clearest.

By 1906 "evolution" had become a promise: history moves towards something better. Zhang
attacks the promise, not the science. Read the passage as a refutation: who holds the view
under attack, what Zhang concedes, where he turns, and how he names his own theory. The
original has no punctuation; ours is added. The omissions are marked …….

## Text

近世言進化論者，蓋昉於海格爾氏。雖無進化之明文，而所謂世界之發展即理性之發展者，進化之説已櫱芽其閒矣。逹爾文、斯賓塞爾輩應用其說，一舉生物見象爲證，一舉社㑹見象爲證，如彼所執，終局目的必達於盡美醇善之區，而進化論始成。……

吾不謂進化之説非也。……若云進化終極必能達於盡美醇善之區，則隨舉一事，無不可以反脣相稽。彼不悟進化之所以為進化者，非由一方直進，而必由雙方並進。專舉一方，惟言智識進化可爾。若以道德言，則善亦進化，惡亦進化；若以生計言，則樂亦進化，苦亦進化。雙方并進，如景之隨形，如罔兩之逐景，非有佗也。

智識愈髙，雖欲舉一廢一而不可得。曩時之善惡爲小，而今之善惡爲大；曩時之苦樂爲小，而今之苦樂爲大。然則以求善求樂爲目的者，果以進化為冣幸邪？其抑以進化爲冣不幸邪？進化之實不可非，而進化之用無所取。自標吾論曰俱分進化論。

## Translation

Those in recent times who speak of the theory of evolution probably start from Hegel. Although he made no explicit statement of evolution, in what he called "the development of the world is the development of reason" the theory of evolution had already sprouted. Darwin, Spencer and others of their kind applied his theory, the one citing the phenomena of living things as proof, the other the phenomena of society; and, according to what they hold, the final goal must reach a realm of perfect beauty and pure goodness. With this the theory of evolution was complete. ……

I do not say that the theory of evolution is wrong. …… But if it is said that evolution must in the end reach a realm of perfect beauty and pure goodness, then any example taken at random will answer it back. What they fail to see is that what makes evolution evolution is not advance straight in one direction, but advance in both directions together. To take one side only would be acceptable only for knowledge. Speaking of morality, good evolves and evil evolves too; speaking of livelihood, pleasure evolves and suffering evolves too. The two advance together, like a shadow following its form, like the penumbra following the shadow; there is nothing else to it.

The higher knowledge rises, the less one can keep one and discard the other, however much one wishes to. In former times good and evil were small, and now good and evil are great; in former times pleasure and suffering were small, and now pleasure and suffering are great. That being so, for those whose goal is to seek goodness and pleasure, is evolution really the greatest good fortune? Or is it rather the greatest misfortune? The reality of evolution cannot be denied, but its value is nil. I label my theory "the theory of evolution in both directions".

## Notes

**The shape of a refutation.**

| Step | Words | Content |
|---|---|---|
| the view attacked | 近世言進化論者……而進化論始成 | its origin (Hegel) and its claim (a final goal of perfect good) |
| concession | 吾不謂進化之説非也 | evolution itself is real |
| the point attacked | 若云……則…… | not evolution, but the claim that it ends in perfect good |
| diagnosis | 彼不悟…… | they fail to see that it advances in both directions |
| evidence | 若以道德言……若以生計言…… | morality and livelihood |
| dilemma | 果……邪？其抑……邪？ | fortune or misfortune? |
| conclusion | 進化之實不可非，而進化之用無所取 | real, but worthless |
| the name | 自標吾論曰…… | "evolution in both directions" |

Compare Yan Fu's comment in [Self-interest and the group](u17-p02-self-interest-and-the-group.md):
both writers grant something before they object. See [Structuring an argument](../../grammar/argument-structure.md).

**實 and 用.** 進化之實不可非，而進化之用無所取 "the reality of evolution cannot be denied, but
its use is nil" plays on the old pairing of reality (實) with function or use (用), like Yan Fu's 體
and 用 in [Struggle and selection](u17-p01-struggle-and-selection.md). For Yan, evolution was the
substance of the world; for Zhang it is a fact without value.

**Allusions.** 罔兩問景 "the penumbra questioned the shadow" is a parable in the *Zhuangzi*
(〈齊物論〉): the faint outer shadow asks the shadow why it cannot stand still, and the shadow
answers that it depends on something else. Zhang uses the image for two things that cannot be
separated. 盡美 recalls the *Analects* (盡美矣，又盡善也), and 反脣相稽 "to purse one's lips and
answer back" comes from the *Hanshu*. See [Allusion](../../grammar/allusion.md).

**Zhang's spelling.** Zhang preferred old character forms, and his collected works keep them:
逹 for 達, 㑹 for 會, 冣 for 最, 髙 for 高, 佗 for 他, 景 for 影. Two forms of the same character
can even appear side by side (説 and 說, 爲 and 為). Learn to see through them; see
[Script and dictionaries](../../toolkit/t01-script-and-dictionaries.md).

**Names.** Zhang writes Hegel 海格爾, Darwin 逹爾文 and, in the omitted passage, Huxley 赫衰黎 and
Schopenhauer 索賓霍爾. Yan Fu's 赫胥黎 is the form that lasted. See
[Transliteration](../../grammar/transliteration.md).

**New and old words.** 進化 "evolution" here is the Japanese term that Wang Guowei preferred to Yan
Fu's 天演. Next to it stand other new terms (發展, 理性, 目的, 社㑹, 見象), all framed in a
style older than Yan Fu's.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

To whom does Zhang trace the theory of evolution?

- [ ] Darwin
- [x] Hegel
- [ ] Spencer

### 2. short-answer

Zhang does not deny evolution. What claim of the evolutionists does he reject?

**Answer:** The claim that evolution must in the end reach a realm of perfect beauty and pure goodness (必達於盡美醇善之區).

### 3. short-answer

According to Zhang, in which field does evolution advance in one direction only? What happens in morality?

**Answer:** Only in knowledge (智識). In morality good and evil both evolve: both are greater now than in former times.

### 4. translate

Translate 進化之實不可非，而進化之用無所取。

**Answer:** The reality of evolution cannot be denied, but its value is nil.

### 5. punctuate

Punctuate this sentence.

**Text:** 若以道德言則善亦進化惡亦進化若以生計言則樂亦進化苦亦進化

**Answer:** 若以道德言，則善亦進化，惡亦進化；若以生計言，則樂亦進化，苦亦進化。
