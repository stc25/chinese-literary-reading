## Introduction

The New Penal Code was drafted in the last years of the Qing on European and Japanese
models, and promulgated in 1911 as the 大清新刑律. In March 1912 the new Republic adopted
it, with changes, as the 暫行新刑律 "Provisional New Penal Code". Most of its articles follow
one simple pattern: "whoever does X shall be sentenced to Y".

## Text

第三百十一條：殺人者，處死刑、無期徒刑或一等有期徒刑。

第二百七十一條：吸食鴉片煙者，處五等有期徒刑、拘役，或一千圓以下罰金。

第二百七十六條：賭博財物者，處一千圓以下罰金。但以供人暫時娛樂之物為賭者，不在此限。

第四十八條：沒收之物如左：一、違禁、私造、私有之物。二、供犯罪所用及預備之物。三、因犯罪所得之物。

## Translation

Article 311: Whoever kills a person shall be sentenced to death, life imprisonment or first-class fixed-term imprisonment.

Article 271: Whoever smokes opium shall be sentenced to fifth-class fixed-term imprisonment, detention, or a fine of up to one thousand dollars.

Article 276: Whoever gambles for money or goods shall be sentenced to a fine of up to one thousand dollars. But this does not apply to those who use as stakes things that only provide people with temporary amusement.

Article 48: Things to be confiscated are as follows: 1. Things that are prohibited, illicitly made or illicitly possessed. 2. Things used or prepared for committing a crime. 3. Things obtained through crime.

## Notes

**者 "whoever…".** 殺人者 "the one who kills a person, whoever kills a person". 者 after a
verb phrase turns it into a noun phrase for the **person who does** the action. Every penal
article here begins this way: 吸食鴉片煙者, 賭博財物者. See
[者: "the one who, that which"](../../grammar/zhe-nominaliser.md).

**所 "what is…".** 所 before a verb makes a noun phrase for the **thing the action is done
to**: 所用 "what is used", 所得 "what is obtained". So 供犯罪所用之物 "things used for committing a crime".
See [所 + verb: "that which"](../../grammar/suo.md).

**者 and 所 compared.**

| | Points to | Example |
|---|---|---|
| verb + 者 | the doer | 殺人者 "one who kills" |
| 所 + verb | the thing done | 所得 "what is obtained" |

**Legal formulas.** 處… "shall be sentenced to…"; 以下 "or less"; 如左 "as follows" (the original was printed
vertically, so the list followed on the left); 但…不在此限 "but … this does not apply", introducing an exception.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

What does **吸食鴉片煙者** mean?

- [ ] opium smoking
- [x] whoever smokes opium
- [ ] the opium that is smoked

**Explanation:** 者 after a verb phrase points to the person who does the action.

### 2. choice

What does **所得**之物 mean?

- [ ] things that obtain
- [x] things that are obtained
- [ ] the obtaining of things

**Explanation:** 所 before a verb points to the thing the action is done to.

### 3. short-answer

What exception does article 276 make?

**Answer:** It does not apply to gambling with things that only provide temporary amusement as stakes.

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the article.

**Text:** 殺人者處死刑無期徒刑或一等有期徒刑

**Answer:** 殺人者，處死刑、無期徒刑或一等有期徒刑。

**Explanation:** 者 closes the description of the offender; 、 separates the list of penalties.

### 5. translate

Translate 吸食鴉片煙者，處五等有期徒刑、拘役，或一千圓以下罰金。

**Answer:** Whoever smokes opium shall be sentenced to fifth-class fixed-term imprisonment, detention, or a fine of up to one thousand dollars.
