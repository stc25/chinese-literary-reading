## Introduction

Paragraph 1 comes from a letter of 1870 in which Zeng Guofan, old and ill, wrote four rules
for his sons. This is the fourth: 習勞則神欽 "work hard, and the spirits will respect you".
軍興以來 "since the war began" refers to the Taiping war of the 1850s–60s.

Paragraph 2 comes from Xue Fucheng's diary, as his ship left Alexandria in early 1890.

## Text

軍興以來，每見人有一材一技、能耐艱苦者，無不見用於人，見稱於時。其絕無材技、不慣作勞者，皆唾棄於時，饑凍就斃。故勤則壽，逸則夭；勤則有材而見用，逸則無能而見棄；勤則博濟斯民，而神祇欽仰，逸則無補於人，而神鬼不歆。是以君子欲為人神所憑依，莫大於習勞也。

有公司行派運淡水來船之小火輪，纜掛船尾未及解放，被撞頃刻沉沒，幸未傷人，足見行海之險。

## Translation

Since the war began, every time I have seen people who have some talent or skill and can endure hardship, without exception they have been employed by others and praised by their contemporaries. Those who have no talent or skill at all and are not used to hard work are all spurned by their contemporaries and perish of hunger and cold. Therefore the diligent live long and the idle die young; the diligent have talent and are employed, the idle have no ability and are cast aside; the diligent help the people widely and the gods respect them, the idle are of no help to anyone and the spirits do not accept their offerings. So, if the noble person wishes to be relied on by people and spirits, nothing is more important than becoming used to hard work.

A small steamboat that the company had sent out to bring fresh water to our ship had its cable caught on our stern; before it could be released, the boat was struck and sank in an instant. Fortunately no one was hurt; but it shows how dangerous seafaring is.

## Notes

**Passives without a passive verb form.** Chinese verbs have no passive form. Often nothing
marks the passive at all: in 唾棄於時 "are spurned by their contemporaries", only 於 "by" and
the sense tell you. But there are also several **markers**:

| Marker | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 見 + verb | 見用, 見棄 | be employed, be cast aside |
| verb + 於 + agent | 見用於人, 唾棄於時 | be employed by others, be spurned by one's contemporaries |
| 為 + agent + 所 + verb | 為人神所憑依 | be relied on by people and spirits |
| 被 + verb | 被撞 | was struck |

See [Passive constructions](../../grammar/passives.md).

**見 jiàn has two jobs here.** In 每見人… "every time (I) see people…", 見 is the verb "to
see". In 見用 and 見棄, it stands before another verb and marks the passive. Glosses separate them with `#n`.

**者 "those who".** 能耐艱苦者 "those who can endure hardship"; 不慣作勞者 "those who are not used to hard work".
Both are long verb phrases closed by 者. See [者: "the one who, that which"](../../grammar/zhe-nominaliser.md).

**Parallel pairs.** 勤則…，逸則… "if diligent then…, if idle then…" is repeated three times.
Parallelism like this helps you divide and understand the text.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

What does **見**棄 mean?

- [ ] to see (someone) abandon
- [x] to be cast aside
- [ ] to cast aside

**Explanation:** 見 before a verb marks the passive.

### 2. choice

In 為人神所憑依, who does the relying?

- [ ] the noble person
- [x] people and spirits
- [ ] no one

**Explanation:** In 為 A 所 V "be V-ed by A", A (人神) is the agent: the noble person is relied on by people and spirits.

### 3. choice

In 被撞頃刻沉沒, what happened to the small steamboat?

- [ ] It struck the ship and sank it.
- [x] It was struck and sank at once.
- [ ] It nearly struck the ship.

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the sentence.

**Text:** 勤則有材而見用逸則無能而見棄

**Answer:** 勤則有材而見用，逸則無能而見棄。

**Explanation:** The two halves are parallel: 勤則… / 逸則….

### 5. translate

Translate 是以君子欲為人神所憑依，莫大於習勞也。

**Answer:** So, if the noble person wishes to be relied on by people and spirits, nothing is more important than becoming used to hard work.
