## Introduction

Four sentences from different essays of Liang Qichao's 〈新民說〉 ("On the New Citizen",
1902–1906), each built around 所以. They come from the essays on public morality (paragraph 2),
on the idea of the state (paragraphs 1 and 3), and on rights (paragraph 4). The last
sentence paraphrases the German jurist Rudolf von Jhering's *Der Kampf ums Recht* (1872),
which uses the image of Justice holding scales and a sword.

## Text

人之所以貴於他物者，以其能群耳。

道德之立，所以利群也。

今吾中國所以日即衰落者，豈有他哉？

衡所以權權利之輕重，劍所以護權利之實行。

## Translation

The reason human beings are nobler than other creatures is simply that they can form societies.

Morality is established as the means of benefiting society.

What else could be the reason why our China is declining day by day?

The scales are the means of weighing rights; the sword is the means of enforcing them.

## Notes

**Two meanings of 所以.** 所以 is 所 + 以 "that by which". It has two related uses:

| Meaning | Example |
|---|---|
| "the means by which" | 衡所以權權利之輕重 "the scales are that by which one weighs rights" |
| "the reason why" | 人之所以貴於他物者 "the reason why human beings are nobler than other creatures" |

In both, 所以 + verb is a noun phrase. See [所以: "the means by which, the reason why"](../../grammar/suoyi.md).

**The frame A 之所以 V 者，B (也/耳).** "The reason why A Vs is B". 之 turns A + 所以… into a
noun phrase (see [之 between subject and verb](../../grammar/zhi-nominaliser.md)), 者 closes it,
and the comment gives the reason. 以其能群耳 "is because they can form societies, that is all": 以 "because", 耳 "only".

**權 twice.** In 衡所以權權利之輕重, the first 權 is a verb, "to weigh", and the second is part
of the new word 權利 "rights". Liang plays on the fact that the same character is used.

**豈有他哉 "how could it be anything else?".** A rhetorical question: the answer, "nothing
else", is implied. See [Rhetorical questions](../../grammar/rhetorical-questions.md).

## Exercises

### 1. choice

In 衡**所以**權權利之輕重, what does 所以 mean?

- [ ] therefore
- [x] that by which, the means of
- [ ] the reason why

**Explanation:** "The scales are that by which (one) weighs the weight of rights."

### 2. choice

What does 人之**所以**貴於他物者 mean?

- [ ] Therefore humans are nobler than other creatures.
- [x] The reason why human beings are nobler than other creatures…
- [ ] Humans are the means of other creatures' nobility.

**Explanation:** 所以…者 here is "the reason why…".

### 3. short-answer

According to the first sentence, why are human beings nobler than other creatures?

**Answer:** Because they can form societies (以其能群耳).

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the sentence.

**Text:** 衡所以權權利之輕重劍所以護權利之實行

**Answer:** 衡所以權權利之輕重，劍所以護權利之實行。

**Explanation:** The two halves are parallel: 衡所以… / 劍所以….

### 5. translate

Translate 道德之立，所以利群也。

**Answer:** Morality is established as the means of benefiting society.
