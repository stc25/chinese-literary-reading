## Introduction

How did a nineteenth-century gentleman learn to write a letter? From models. Collections of
letters (尺牘 chǐdú, literally "a foot of writing tablet") by admired writers were printed and
reprinted as manuals, often with annotations explaining every allusion. The most popular of all
was 《秋水軒尺牘》 "Letters from the Autumn Water Studio" by Xu Simei, a private secretary (幕友)
from Shaoxing who spent his life working for officials in the north. It was still a bestseller,
in annotated editions, in the 1930s.

Read two of his letters to friends: the first written after a brief meeting, the second
consoling a friend whose brother has died. Xu writes between equals, so he lowers himself (弟)
and raises his friend (大兄, 足下, 令弟, 尊體) but not as steeply as Sun Yat-sen does to Li
Hongzhang. Almost every phrase is an allusion; the glosses explain them.

## Text

弟向獲締交於季方，因得聞元方之賢，思一見爲快。昨於會城邂逅遇之，覺大兄之才華器宇，更有勝於所聞者。正恨相見之晚，不期越宿分襟，又恨相違之速矣。別後初四日抵津門，初十日詣平舒，月未一圓，地經兩易。風塵僕僕，無非芸人之田，自憐亦堪自笑。

比值同人歸里，館中惟我獨居；加以清磬紅魚，直是修行古剎。而西風黃葉，洄溯時殷，雙鯉之頒，定不我棄。尊體復元否？嫁務紛勞，諸宜珍攝。因風寄意，不盡所懷。

久不見碧梧翠竹之姿，每於月白風清，輒深神往。且以足下久不入直爲疑。近得阮昔侯札，知駕遊山左，種蓮楚邱者，已非一日。青萍結綠，到處爭售，所固然也。

令弟才華意氣，滿謂嵆山獨鶴，足以振采雞羣；何期牙琴一摧，墓草已宿，令人感慨係之！

弟初客遼西，旋遊津淀。今春復有平舒之役。年年壓線，依舊幫傭，良由村女蛾眉，難爲時賞耳。

## Translation

I, your younger brother, formerly had the good fortune to make friends with your younger brother, and so heard of your worth as the elder, and longed for the pleasure of meeting you. Yesterday I met you by chance in the provincial capital, and felt that your talent and bearing surpassed even what I had heard. Just as I was regretting that we had not met sooner, we unexpectedly parted after a single night, and now I regret that we were separated so soon. After we parted, I reached Tianjin on the fourth and went to Pingshu on the tenth: in less than a month I have changed place twice. Travel-worn, I do nothing but weed other people's fields; I pity myself, and can also laugh at myself.

Just now my colleagues have gone home, and I live alone in the office; add the clear chime and the red wooden fish, and it is just like an old temple for religious practice. With the west wind and the yellow leaves, I often long for you deeply; you will surely not abandon me, but will send me a letter. Have you recovered your health? With the wedding arrangements so many and tiring, please take good care of yourself. I send my thoughts by the wind, and cannot express all I feel.

I have long not seen your bearing, like green parasol trees and jade bamboo; whenever the moon is bright and the wind clear, I long for you in spirit. I had wondered why you had not been on duty for so long. Recently I received a letter from Ruan Xihou and learnt that you have travelled to Shandong and have long been serving in a secretariat at Chuqiu. Fine swords and fine jade are sought after everywhere: that is only to be expected.

Your younger brother's talent and spirit, I was sure, like Ji Shao, the lone crane, would shine among the flock of chickens. Who could have expected that Boya's zither would be broken, and that the grass on the grave is already a year old? It moves one to sorrow!

I, your younger brother, first lived in Liaoxi, then soon moved to Tianjin and its lakes. This spring I have again taken up a post at Pingshu. Year after year I sew for others, still a hired hand: truly because a village girl's fine eyebrows are hard for the age to admire.

## Notes

**Between equals.** Friends call themselves 弟 "younger brother" and each other 兄 or 大兄 "elder
brother", whatever their ages. 足下 "(at) your feet" is the standard polite "you" between equals.
Things belonging to the other are honoured with 令 (令弟 "your younger brother") or 尊 (尊體
"your health"); his travels are 駕 "your carriage". Compare the steep language of a petition in
[A petition to Li Hongzhang](u19-p01-a-petition-to-li-hongzhang.md). See
[Honorific and humble forms](../../grammar/honorific-humble.md).

**The closing.** 諸宜珍攝 "please take good care of yourself", 因風寄意 "I send my thoughts by the
wind", 不盡所懷 "I cannot say all I feel" are standard closing formulas. Printed collections
usually omit the address, the date and the signature, which every writer knew how to add. See
[Letter formulas](../../grammar/epistolary-formulas.md).

**Allusion as politeness.** A 尺牘 says little directly. The friend's brothers are 元方 and 季方, the
sons of the Han official Chen Shi 陳寔, whose virtues were so equal that it was hard to say which
was better (《世說新語》: 元方難為兄，季方難為弟). A letter is 雙鯉, from the old ballad of a guest
who brings a pair of carp with a letter inside. Death is 牙琴一摧: when Zhong Ziqi died, Boya broke
his zither, because no one else understood his music. 墓草已宿 recalls the *Book of Rites*: one does
not weep at a friend's grave once the grass is a year old. The allusions flatter the reader, who is
assumed to know them all.

**A secretary's complaint.** Xu was a 幕友, a private secretary paid by officials, moving from post to
post. He describes his work with two images of labour for others: 芸人之田 "weeding other people's
fields" (Mencius: 人病舍其田而芸人之田) and 年年壓線 "year after year sewing", from the Tang poem
〈貧女〉 by Qin Taoyu, where a poor girl sews wedding clothes for other brides. 村女蛾眉，難爲時賞 "a
village girl's beauty is hard for the age to admire" ends the same image: his talent goes unnoticed.

**Places.** Xu worked in Zhili (modern Hebei) and around Tianjin: 津門 and 津淀 are Tianjin, 平舒 is
the old name of Daicheng, 會城 the provincial capital (Baoding). Letters often use old or literary
place names.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

Who is 季方 in the first letter?

- [x] the younger brother of the recipient
- [ ] the writer himself
- [ ] a son of the writer

### 2. short-answer

Find three words or phrases that raise the recipient or his family.

**Answer:** Any three of: 大兄, 尊體, 足下, 駕, 令弟, 碧梧翠竹之姿, and the comparison with 元方.

### 3. short-answer

What sad news does the second letter (to Zhao Nanhu) refer to, and how is the death expressed?

**Answer:** The recipient's younger brother has died, about a year ago. The death is expressed by allusions: Boya's zither broken (牙琴一摧) and the grass on the grave a year old (墓草已宿).

### 4. translate

Translate 尊體復元否？嫁務紛勞，諸宜珍攝。

**Answer:** Have you recovered your health? With the wedding arrangements so many and tiring, please take good care of yourself.

Punctuate this closing.

**Answer:** 因風寄意，不盡所懷。
