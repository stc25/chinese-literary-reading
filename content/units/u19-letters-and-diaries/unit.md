## Introduction

Formal letters and diaries are full of polite formulas that carry social meaning.

## Objectives

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- identify the parts of a formal letter
- read honorific and humble forms
- read diary entries

## Passages

1. A formal letter (planned)
2. 翁同龢日記: entries (planned)
3. A letter-writing manual (尺牘): a model letter (planned)
