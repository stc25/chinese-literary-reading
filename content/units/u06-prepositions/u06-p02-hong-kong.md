## Introduction

Article 3 of the Treaty of Nanjing cedes the island of Hong Kong to Britain. It begins by
giving a reason (因… "because…") and then states the cession itself. The text keeps two
variant characters of the printed treaty: 徃 for 往 and 㨿 for 據.

## Text

一、因大英商船遠路涉洋，徃徃有損壞須修補者，自應給予沿海一處，以便修船及存守所用物料。今大皇帝准將香港一島給予大英君主暨嗣後世襲主位者常遠㨿守主掌，任便立法治理。

## Translation

Item. Because British merchant ships travel long distances across the ocean, and some are often damaged and need repair, they should naturally be given a place on the coast so that they can repair their ships and store the materials they use. The Great Emperor now agrees to give the island of Hong Kong to the Sovereign of Great Britain and to the heirs and successors to the throne, to be held and ruled permanently, and to make laws and govern it at their discretion.

## Notes

**以便 "so that".** 以 introduces the purpose of an action as well as its means. 以便
"so as to make it convenient to…" is a fixed expression for "so that, in order to": 以便修船
"so that (they) can repair ships". See [以: "by means of, with"](../../grammar/yi-preposition.md).

**自 is not always "from".** In 自應 "naturally should", 自 means "of itself,
naturally". Compare 自今以後 "from now on" in [Peace and five ports](u06-p01-peace-and-five-ports.md).
When 自 stands directly before a modal or verb (應, 行, 定), suspect "of itself, by oneself".

**將 + object + verb.** 准將香港一島給予大英君主 is "agrees to take the island of Hong
Kong and give it to the British sovereign". 將 brings the object (香港一島) **before** the
verb (給予). This is common in documents of the period, and it makes long objects easier to read.

**Variant characters.** 徃 is a variant of 往, and 㨿 of 據. Printed texts of the period use many such forms;
the glosses give the common form after "=". See [Script and dictionaries](../../toolkit/t01-script-and-dictionaries.md).

**有…者 "there are some that…".** 徃徃有損壞須修補者 "often there are (ships) that are
damaged and need repair". 者 turns the verb phrase into "those that…", and 有 says such ships exist.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

What does **以便**修船 mean?

- [ ] by means of repairing ships
- [x] so that they can repair ships
- [ ] after repairing ships

**Explanation:** 以便 introduces a purpose: "so that, in order to".

### 2. choice

In **自**應給予沿海一處, what does 自 mean?

- [ ] from
- [x] naturally, as a matter of course
- [ ] oneself

**Explanation:** Before the modal 應 "should", 自 means "naturally". It is not the preposition "from" here.

### 3. short-answer

What reason does article 3 give for ceding a place on the coast?

**Answer:** British merchant ships travel long distances across the ocean and are often damaged, so they need a place to repair ships and store materials.

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the sentence.

**Text:** 因大英商船遠路涉洋徃徃有損壞須修補者自應給予沿海一處以便修船及存守所用物料

**Answer:** 因大英商船遠路涉洋，徃徃有損壞須修補者，自應給予沿海一處，以便修船及存守所用物料。

**Explanation:** The sentence moves from reason (因…), through need (…者), to consequence (自應…) and purpose (以便…). Each step gets its own clause.

### 5. translate

Translate 今大皇帝准將香港一島給予大英君主。

**Answer:** The Great Emperor now agrees to give the island of Hong Kong to the Sovereign of Great Britain.
