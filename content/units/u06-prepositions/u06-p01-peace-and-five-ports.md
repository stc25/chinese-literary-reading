## Introduction

The Treaty of Nanjing (南京條約, also 江寧條約) ended the First Opium War between the Qing
empire and Britain. It was signed on 29 August 1842 (道光二十二年七月二十四日) aboard a
British warship off Nanjing. These are its first two articles: perpetual peace, and the
opening of five ports to British residence and trade. Every article of the treaty begins
with 一、 "item", not with a number. The second article continues after the point where this excerpt stops.

## Text

一、嗣後大清大皇帝、大英君主永存平和，所屬華英人民彼此友睦，各住他國者必受該國保佑身家全安。

一、自今以後大皇帝恩准大英國人民帶同所屬家眷，寄居大清沿海之廣州、福州、廈門、寧波、上海等五處港口，貿易通商無礙；且大英君主派設領事、管事等官住該五處城邑，專理商賈事宜，與各該地方官公文往來；……

## Translation

Item. Hereafter the Great Emperor of the Great Qing and the Sovereign of Great Britain shall maintain peace forever, and their Chinese and British subjects shall be friendly to each other; those who live in the other's country shall receive that country's protection, with complete safety for their persons and families.

Item. From now on the Great Emperor graciously permits British subjects, with their families, to reside at the five ports of Canton, Fuzhou, Amoy, Ningbo and Shanghai on the coast of the Great Qing, and to trade there without hindrance; moreover the Sovereign of Great Britain shall appoint consuls, superintendents and other officials to live in these five cities, to deal specifically with the affairs of merchants, and to exchange official documents with the local officials of each place; …

## Notes

**Prepositions.** Literary Chinese prepositions are words that, like English "from" and
"with", introduce a noun and tell you its role. Many are also verbs in other contexts.

- **自 "from"**: 自今以後 "from now (on)". See [自 and 由: "from"](../../grammar/zi-you-from.md).
- **與 "with"**: 與各該地方官公文往來 "exchange official documents **with** the local officials".
  The phrase with 與 comes **before** the verb (往來). See [與: "with, and"](../../grammar/yu-with.md).

Notice where the phrase stands: in literary Chinese a prepositional phrase normally comes
**before** the verb it goes with, where English puts it after.

**等 closing a list.** 廣州、福州、廈門、寧波、上海等五處 "Canton, Fuzhou, Amoy, Ningbo,
Shanghai: (these) five places". 等 after a list can mean "and so on", but when a number
follows, as here, the list is complete.

**Diplomatic language.** The emperor 恩准 "graciously permits", but the sovereign of
Britain is named on equal terms (大清大皇帝、大英君主). The treaty's wording on rank was
itself a matter of negotiation. Article 11 sets rules for how the two sides address each other.

## Exercises

### 1. choice

What does **自**今以後 mean?

- [x] from now on
- [ ] naturally, from now
- [ ] by oneself, from now

**Explanation:** 自 is the preposition "from"; 自今以後 "from now and after".

### 2. choice

In 與各該地方官公文往來, where does the phrase with 與 stand?

- [x] before the verb 往來
- [ ] after the verb 往來
- [ ] at the end of the sentence

**Explanation:** Prepositional phrases normally stand before the verb in literary Chinese: "with the local officials, exchange documents".

### 3. short-answer

Which five ports are opened by article 2?

**Answer:** 廣州 Canton, 福州 Fuzhou, 廈門 Amoy, 寧波 Ningbo and 上海 Shanghai.

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the sentence.

**Text:** 所屬華英人民彼此友睦各住他國者必受該國保佑身家全安

**Answer:** 所屬華英人民彼此友睦，各住他國者必受該國保佑身家全安。

### 5. translate

Translate 嗣後大清大皇帝、大英君主永存平和。

**Answer:** Hereafter the Great Emperor of the Great Qing and the Sovereign of Great Britain shall maintain peace forever.
