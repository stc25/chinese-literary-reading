## Introduction

Three money clauses of the Treaty of Nanjing. Article 4 refers to the events of 1839, when
the imperial commissioner Lin Zexu 林則徐 confined the foreign community in Canton and
forced the surrender and destruction of their opium. The end of article 5 deals with the
debts of the Canton merchant guild, and part of article 7 sets the total and term of payment.

## Text

一、因大清欽差大憲等於道光十九年二月間經將大英國領事官及民人等強留粵省，赫以死罪，索出鴉片以為贖命，今大皇帝准以洋銀六百萬圓償補原價。

一、……今酌定洋銀三百萬圓，作為商欠之數，准明由中國官為償還。

一、……自壬寅年起至乙巳年止，四年共交銀二千一百萬圓。

## Translation

Item. Because in the second month of the nineteenth year of Daoguang (1839) the imperial commissioner and other high officials of the Great Qing detained the British consul and British subjects by force in Guangdong, threatened them with death, and demanded the surrender of their opium as ransom for their lives, the Great Emperor now agrees to pay six million silver dollars to compensate for its original value.

Item. … Three million silver dollars are now fixed as the amount of the merchants' debts, and it is expressly agreed that the Chinese officials will repay them on the merchants' behalf.

Item. … From the renyin year to the yisi year, over four years, a total of twenty-one million dollars in silver shall be paid.

## Notes

**Five prepositions in one passage.**

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 於 | at, in (time) | 於道光十九年二月間 "in the second month of the 19th year of Daoguang" |
| 以 | with, by means of | 以洋銀六百萬圓償補 "compensate with six million dollars" |
| 由 | by (who does it) | 由中國官…償還 "repaid by the Chinese officials" |
| 為 (wèi) | for, on behalf of | 為償還 "repay on (their) behalf" |
| 自…至… | from … to … | 自壬寅年起至乙巳年止 "from the renyin year to the yisi year" |

As in [Peace and five ports](u06-p01-peace-and-five-ports.md), each phrase stands before its verb.

**A trap: 等於.** In 大憲等於道光十九年, the characters 等 and 於 are next to each other, but
they do not form the word 等於 "to equal". 等 closes the list of people ("the high officials
**and others**"), and 於 begins a new phrase ("**in** the 19th year of Daoguang"). Always ask
where one phrase ends and the next begins.

**以 three times.** 赫以死罪 "threatened them **with** death"; 以為贖命 "**as** ransom";
准以洋銀…償補 "agrees to compensate **with** silver dollars". See
[以: "by means of, with"](../../grammar/yi-preposition.md).

**Dates.** 道光十九年 is the 19th year of the Daoguang emperor's reign, i.e. 1839. 壬寅 and
乙巳 are years of the sixty-year cycle, here 1842 and 1845. See
[Dates and calendars](../../toolkit/t02-dates-and-calendars.md).

## Exercises

### 1. choice

In **於**道光十九年二月間, what does 於 mean?

- [x] in, at (a time)
- [ ] equal to (as in 等於)
- [ ] than

**Explanation:** 於 begins a time phrase here. 等 belongs to the list before it ("the high officials and others").

### 2. choice

In which phrase does 以 mean "as"?

- [ ] 赫以死罪
- [x] 索出鴉片以為贖命
- [ ] 准以洋銀六百萬圓償補原價

**Explanation:** 以為 "taking (it) as": the opium was demanded "as" ransom. In the other two, 以 means "with, by means of".

### 3. choice

In 由中國官**為**償還, what does 為 mean?

- [ ] to be
- [x] for, on behalf of
- [ ] because of

**Explanation:** Read wèi, 為 is a preposition: the officials repay on behalf of the merchants.

### 4. short-answer

Over which Western years were the payments to be made?

**Answer:** From 1842 (壬寅) to 1845 (乙巳).

### 5. punctuate

Punctuate the sentence.

**Text:** 自壬寅年起至乙巳年止四年共交銀二千一百萬圓

**Answer:** 自壬寅年起至乙巳年止，四年共交銀二千一百萬圓。
