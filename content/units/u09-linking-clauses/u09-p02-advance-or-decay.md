## Introduction

Four sentences from later in the same preface. Liang argues that a dynasty decays if it
lets its institutions drift, and is restored if it reforms them. He supports this with a line
from the Book of Odes (詩經) and with four homely comparisons. Each paragraph here is a separate sentence from the preface.

## Text

委心任運，聽其流變，則日趨於敝；振刷整頓，斟酌通變，則日趨于善。

苟其子孫達於此義，自審其敝而自變之，斯號中興矣。

《詩》曰：「周雖舊邦，其命維新。」言治舊國必用新法也。

夜不炳燭則昧，冬不御裘則寒，渡河而乘陸車者危，易證而嘗舊方者死。

## Translation

If one gives up, leaves things to fate and lets them drift and change, then day by day they tend towards decay; if one rouses and reorganises, weighs things carefully and adapts to change, then day by day they tend towards good.

If their descendants understand this principle, examine the decay themselves and change it themselves, then this is called a restoration.

The Odes say: "Though Zhou is an old state, its mandate is new." This means that to govern an old country one must use new methods.

At night, if you do not light a candle, it is dark; in winter, if you do not wear furs, it is cold; one who crosses a river riding a land carriage is in danger; one who takes the old prescription when the illness has changed dies.

## Notes

**Conditions without "if".** In 委心任運，聽其流變，則日趨於敝 there is no word for "if":
the first clauses state a condition, and 則 "then" introduces the result. Likewise 夜不炳燭則昧
"(if) at night (you) do not light a candle, then (it is) dark". This "A 則 B" pattern is one
of the most common in literary prose. See [則: "then, in that case"](../../grammar/ze.md).

**苟 "if".** When the condition is marked, it is with words such as 苟, 若, 如, 使 or 倘: 苟其子孫達於此義…
"if their descendants understand this principle…". The result is introduced by 則 or, as
here, by 斯 "then". See [Conditions](../../grammar/conditionals.md).

**雖 "although".** 周雖舊邦，其命維新 "though Zhou is an old state, its mandate is new". 雖
stands after the subject (周) and introduces what is conceded. See [Concession: 雖, 縱](../../grammar/concessives.md).

**而 "and" and "and yet".** 自審其敝而自變之 "examine the decay **and** change it themselves";
渡河而乘陸車者 "one who crosses a river **and yet** rides a land carriage". Whether 而 means
"and" or "but" depends on how the two parts relate. See [而: linking verbs and clauses](../../grammar/er-conjunction.md).

**The quotation.** 周雖舊邦，其命維新 is from the ode 文王 in the Book of Odes, about the Zhou
dynasty's founder. Reformers quoted it constantly to argue that an old state could renew itself. See
[Allusion and quotation](../../grammar/allusion.md).
