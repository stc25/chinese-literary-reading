## Introduction

Arguments are built from linked clauses: and, but, therefore, if, although. The passages come from two founding texts of the reform movement: Liang Qichao's preface to 〈變法通議〉 (1896), published in the journal 時務報, and Yan Fu's 〈原強〉 (1895).

## Objectives

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

- read 而 and 則 in context
- identify conditions, concessions and causes
- follow the steps of a short argument
