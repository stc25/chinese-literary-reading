## Introduction

Yan Fu 嚴復 (1854–1921) studied at the Royal Naval College in Greenwich and became the
best-known translator of Western thought into Chinese. His essay 〈原強〉 ("On the Origins
of Strength"), written after China's defeat by Japan in 1895, introduced Darwin and Herbert
Spencer to Chinese readers. Here he summarises Darwin's "struggle for existence" (爭自存).
The sentence between the two paragraphs is left out.

## Text

其始也，種與種爭，及其成群成國，則群與群爭，國與國爭。而弱者當為強肉，愚者當為智役焉。

習於安者，使之處勞，狙於山者，使之居澤，不再傳而其種盡矣。爭存之事，如是而已。

## Translation

At first, species struggle with species; when they come to form groups and states, then group struggles with group and state with state. And so the weak are bound to become meat for the strong, and the foolish servants of the clever.

Take those used to ease and make them live in hardship; take those used to the mountains and make them live in marshes: before two generations have passed, their kind will have died out. The struggle for existence is just this.

## Notes

**則 in time.** 及其成群成國，則群與群爭 "when (it comes to) their forming groups and states,
**then** group struggles with group". 則 here links a stage in time to what happens at that
stage, rather than a condition to its result. See [則: "then, in that case"](../../grammar/ze.md).

**而 "and so".** 而弱者當為強肉 "**and so** the weak are bound to become meat for the strong";
不再傳而其種盡矣 "(it is) not passed on a second time **and** the kind dies out". 而 links
the two parts; the meaning of the link comes from the context. See [而: linking verbs and clauses](../../grammar/er-conjunction.md).

**使 "to make".** 使之處勞 "make them live in hardship". 使 + person + verb is the causative
"make someone do something". 使 can also mean "if": see [Conditions](../../grammar/conditionals.md).

**Topic and condition.** 習於安者，使之處勞 is literally "those used to ease, make them live in
hardship": the topic (習於安者) comes first, and the comment is an instruction. In English, a condition
reads best: "if those used to ease are made to live in hardship…".

**New vocabulary.** 爭存 (or 爭自存) "struggle for existence" and 群 "society" were key terms in
Yan Fu's and Liang Qichao's writing. See [Thought and translation](../u17-thought-and-translation/unit.md).

### 2. choice

What does 使**之**處勞 mean?

- [ ] They make hardship.
- [x] Make them live in hardship.
- [ ] If they live in hardship…

**Explanation:** 使 + person + verb is causative: "make (them) do something".

### 3. short-answer

What happens, according to Yan Fu, to creatures used to ease when they are made to live in hardship?

**Answer:** Before two generations have passed, their kind dies out (不再傳而其種盡矣).
