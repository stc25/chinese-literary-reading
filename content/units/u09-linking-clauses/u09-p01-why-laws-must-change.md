## Introduction

〈變法通議〉 ("General Discussion of Reform") was a series of essays by Liang Qichao,
published from 1896 in the reformist journal 時務報 in Shanghai. Its preface opens with a
question and a sweeping answer: everything in the world changes, and so must institutions.
Liang's long list of examples (the formation of the earth, the evolution of animals, the
circulation of the blood) is left out at ……. 法 here means the laws and institutions of the state.

## Text

法何以必變？凡在天地之間者莫不變：晝夜變而成日；寒暑變而成歲；……藉曰不變，則天地人類并時而息矣。故夫變者，古今之公理也。

## Translation

Why must laws change? Everything between heaven and earth changes: day and night change and so make a day; cold and heat change and so make a year; … Supposing they did not change, heaven, earth and humankind would all cease at once. Therefore change is the universal principle of past and present.

## Notes

**而 "and (so)".** 晝夜變而成日 "day and night change, **and so** make a day". 而 links two
verb phrases. Usually the first leads to, or happens before, the second. It can also mean "and yet", as in
[Advance or decay](u09-p02-advance-or-decay.md). See [而: linking verbs and clauses](../../grammar/er-conjunction.md).

**則 "then".** 藉曰不變，則天地人類并時而息矣 "supposing they did not change, **then**
heaven, earth and humankind would cease". 則 introduces the consequence of a condition.
The condition itself is marked here by 藉曰 "supposing"; often there is no "if" at all. See
[則: "then, in that case"](../../grammar/ze.md).

**故 "therefore".** 故夫變者，古今之公理也 draws the conclusion, as a definition (者…也). See
[Cause and result](../../grammar/causal.md).

**莫不 "none … not".** 凡在天地之間者莫不變 "of all things between heaven and earth, there
is none that does not change". Two negatives make a strong positive: "everything changes".

## Exercises

### 1. choice

In 晝夜變**而**成日, what does 而 do?

- [ ] It means "but".
- [x] It links two verb phrases: change, and so make a day.
- [ ] It marks a condition.

### 2. choice

In 藉曰不變，**則**天地人類并時而息矣, what does 則 introduce?

- [ ] a reason
- [x] the consequence of a condition: "then…"
- [ ] a contrast

### 3. choice

What does 凡在天地之間者**莫不**變 mean?

- [ ] Nothing between heaven and earth changes.
- [x] Everything between heaven and earth changes.
- [ ] Some things between heaven and earth do not change.

**Explanation:** 莫不 "there is none that does not" makes a strong positive.

### 4. punctuate

Punctuate the sentence.

**Text:** 故夫變者古今之公理也

**Answer:** 故夫變者，古今之公理也。

**Explanation:** 者 closes the term being defined.

### 5. translate

Translate 藉曰不變，則天地人類并時而息矣。

**Answer:** Supposing they did not change, heaven, earth and humankind would all cease at once.
