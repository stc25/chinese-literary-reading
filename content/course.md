## Introduction

This course teaches you to read the literary Chinese (文言) of the late Qing and Republican periods, roughly 1800–1930, for use in your own research. It assumes no knowledge of Chinese. All explanations are in English, and every reading passage is taken from a text of the period.

## How to use this course

The beginner units build up the core grammar step by step and are best taken in order. The intermediate units each focus on one kind of text and can be taken in any order once you have finished the beginner units, or are confident with their grammar. If you already read some literary Chinese, use the beginner units as a refresher or skip them. The toolkit pages are reference material you can use at any time.
