Accessibility statement

This statement applies to the website {{ course.title }}, run by the Language Centre of the University of Cambridge. We want as many people as possible to be able to use it. You should be able to:

  • zoom in up to 400% without the text spilling off the screen;
  • use the whole site with a keyboard, including the glosses in the reading passages (Tab into the text, move between words with the arrow keys, press Enter to open a gloss and Escape to close it);
  • use the site with a screen reader: Chinese text is marked as traditional Chinese, so a screen reader with a Chinese voice can read it;
  • use the site in dark mode, and with JavaScript switched off (glosses are then shown in tables, and answers to exercises under "Show answer").

How accessible this website is

The site is being developed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. It has been checked with automated tests (structure, labels, headings, colour contrast) and by hand with a keyboard, at phone, tablet and desktop sizes, and in light and dark mode. It has not yet had a full audit with assistive technology. We know of these limitations:

  • Vertical (top-to-bottom, right-to-left) text, offered as an option in some passages, may be read in an unexpected order by some screen readers. The same text is always available in horizontal layout.
  • A small number of rare or old character forms may be shown in a fallback font.
  • Most teaching content is still a draft awaiting review, and is labelled as such.

Enforcement procedure

The Equality and Human Rights Commission (EHRC) is responsible for enforcing the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018. If you are not happy with how we respond to your complaint, contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS).

Preparation of this statement
