What is in the course

  • Units

    {{ counts.units }} units in two levels, with {{ counts.passages }} reading passages taken from texts of the period. The beginner units build up the core grammar step by step. The intermediate units each take one kind of text, such as memorials, treaties, newspapers or letters.

  • Reading with glosses

    Select an underlined word in a passage to see its gloss and pinyin, and a link to the grammar point behind it. Switch between punctuated, unpunctuated and vertical layouts, and try the exercises after each passage.

  • Grammar

    {{ counts.grammar }} short reference pages on the structures of literary Chinese, grouped by the unit that introduces them.

  • Glossary

    {{ "{:,}".format(counts.terms) }} terms from every passage, searchable by character, pinyin or English.

  • Toolkit

    Reference pages to use at any time, including a converter between Qing reign years, Republican years and Western dates.

  • Reading assistant

    Paste a text of your own for help with punctuation, unfamiliar words, grammar, names and allusions, and a draft translation, built on the course glossary and grammar pages. AI suggestions are marked as such, and a help page explains how to check them.
